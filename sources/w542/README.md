# Capitals from W.542

Walters Art Museum, Baltimore, Ms. W.542, the
Silver Gospels (OPenn, the same terms as W.541,
`../README.md`). Its hand and its red initials are
close to those of W.541. Two capitals that W.541
never writes as an initial (`../w541/README.md`,
step 10) are taken from it, one copy each:

| File | Capital | Image | Where |
|------|---------|-------|-------|
| `capitals/054c.png` | Ռ | 34 | «Ռոբովամ ծնաւ զԱբիայ» (Matt. 1:7) |
| `capitals/0551.png` | Ց | 415 | «Ցանկանալով ցանկացայ» (Luke 22:15) |

How they were found: the 573 medium images (sap)
were cut for red initials like W.541's and lined
up with the 1895 Gospels; at the sentences that
start with Ղ Ջ Ռ Ր Ց the initials there were shown
to the user, who named these two. The pictures are
the red ink of the 600 dpi masters (red over green
above the paper's, smoothed; Ռ's broken tail
closed), as large as written, black on white, like
`../w541/capitals/`. Ղ Ջ Ր are not initials in
W.542 either, at the sentences looked at.
