# Anania Shirakatsi: notes

Works in `books/grabar/anania-shirakatsi/`, from
Digilib (ids in `books/SOURCES.md`). Translated so
far (machine, 2026-11-13): `4`, `8`, `13`, `14`,
`16`. Not yet: the others (the Easter sermon, the
History, the Geography ...).

Choices for every part:

- Lines of Roman numerals (`Ա`, `ԺԲ`, ...) and
  numbers in the text (`ՃՀ`, `Մ`) are copied as
  they are.
- Digilib's page marks `/202b/` are not kept in the
  translation (Latin letters); they stay in the
  Grabar.
- Digilib's editorial `< >`, `[ ]` and `//` are not
  copied.

## 4 · Յիշատակարան

A colophon of five lines, damaged in the source
(`...`, kept). `քանիաւնութիւն`: read as "reckoning"
(հաշվարկ). `ի զեղջէ Անէնից`: "from the village of
Anen" (Անենց գյուղից), a guess.

## 8 · Մղոնաչափք եւ Օդաչափք

Distances in `ասպարէզ` (stadion), `քայլ`, `ոտն`,
`մատն`, `մղոն`, and the stations from Dvin (written
`Դունա`; read as Dvin, not translated) to Karin, Rome,
Jerusalem and others. Place names are given in modern
spelling where known; `Պարսահաեան` (sea) is kept as it
is. In the second half, the distances to the spheres
of the planets: `կէս`, `երրեակ`, `հնգեակ`, `տասնեակ`
(fractions) are kept as words. `Ակին` (a sphere) and
`Փայլածու`, `Լուսաբեր`, `Հրատ`, `Լուսնթագ` are the
planet names as the Grabar has them. The line
`Թէ հաւատոյ ոք վիպասանիս` is unclear (best reading:
"if anyone believes the storyteller"). The last
paragraph is a dedication note to a reader, with
corrupt names (`վեր/?/ծանեալ`, `Պտղոմեա/?/կան`).

## 13 · Յաղագս ականց պատուականաց

A list of 33 gems and their colors, `Ա`–`ԼԳ`, ending
with `[Վճարումն Անանիայի]`. Colors are given by the
modern names; `լրջագոյն`, `շիկագոյն` and `Գոճազմն`
are unclear. Gem names are in modern spelling.

## 14 · Յաղագս շաղկապութեան արեգական եւ լուսնի

A calculation recipe (eclipses of the sun and moon),
with Armenian letter numerals. It is cut off at the
end (`եւ այս է`) on Digilib. `բժան`, `նապատիկ` and
`Լպատիկ` are unclear; read as "multiply by" and a
proper name.

## 16 · Յաղագս յափոց, չափուց եւ պատարաց

Eighteen vessels and measures (`Ա`–`ԺԸ`): քսեստ,
կոտիւղ, տրիւղիոն, բիսին, ... քոռ, կիւաթոս.
Their names are kept as words; the units follow the
Grabar (`լտեր` is written `լիտր`). In `Ը` the text
has a Latin `C` (`ընդ C բաժանելոյ`), left out of the
translation. `ԺԸ` says `կադամադէկաւթ` and
`մասմարաւթ` are not translated from the Hebrew.
