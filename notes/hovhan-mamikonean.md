## Translation

Lines 1–654 (Zenob's part) are in
`books/ashkharhabar/.../01.txt`, made 2026-11-22,
`machine`. Hovhan's part (from line 655) is next:
translate it into the same file, line for line.

Choices:

- Verse numbers start the lines as in the Grabar;
  a `*` with no number (where Digilib lost it) is
  kept the same way.
- `[Ա]` lines keep the bracket and translate the
  title; `[Ա ]` lines (with the space) too, in
  capitals. The footnote numbers in the [Դ] title
  (`1`, `2`) are kept.
- `Ç…È` in the Grabar are `«…»` in the translation,
  so the check counts 33 "extra marks": 39 of them,
  less 3 bracketed letters (`[ց]`, `[չ]`, `[ե]`) the
  translation leaves out.
- Numbers in letters: words in running text, digits
  in the list of villages (lines 454–460), where
  they are counts of households and soldiers.
- Names in their modern form: Կրոնիդես (Կրաւնիդէս),
  Ղևոնդիոս (Ղեւոնդէս), Եդեսիա (Ուռհայ), Մանազկերտ
  (Մանծկերտ), Ողական (Ողկան), Չենք (Ճենք).

## Notes on the text

- The numbering slips are Digilib's (or the
  edition's), kept as they are: 69 at line 128 covers
  three verses, then 72, 71, 72 (lines 130–132); 99
  twice (lines 168, 170).
- Line 170 starts in the middle of a sentence
  (`* դուք մի՛ ինչ աղարտէք`), and so do several `*`
  lines with no number (207, 216, 227, 241, ...): the
  first words are lost in Digilib's text.
- Line 241: `* ի Կեսարիայէ՝ մնայր` is translated
  `Կեսարիայից (ելնելով)՝`.
- Line 463: `Յ ամս Լ եւ ը[ե]թ` is read 338 (the
  year Grigor ordered the villages); the last word
  is garbled.
- Line 583: the dead at Թակարդք (1006 + 2201 + 606)
  do not add up to the total, 4112; translated as
  written.
- Line 537: ՃՌՆԾ is read 100 450.
- Line 9: `Մեգաղու` is read Մեծ (Ղևոնդիոս Մեծ).
- Line 650: `գազպէն`, a manna that falls on the
  forests (a kind of honeydew), kept as the word.
