# Armenian authors before the 15th century

Authors known by name (and the main anonymous works)
from the 5th century to 1400, with their books.
Written from general knowledge, not checked against a
catalogue: dates are the usual ones and many are
approximate; a "?" marks a doubtful attribution or
date. **[lib]** means the author is in `books/grabar/`
(folder name in brackets). "Lost" means known only
from a mention.

## Before the alphabet (not Armenian texts)

Nothing written in Armenian survives before the
alphabet of Mesrop Mashtots (405). Earlier voices
are in other languages: Greek and Syriac writers
who mention Armenians, and oral epics later written
down (Sasna Tsrer is much later; the songs of
Goghtn are quoted by Khorenatsi).

## 5th century

- **Mesrop Mashtots** (c. 362-440): the alphabet
  (405); the first translations; prayers and
  letters (?). Life: Koriun.
- **Sahak Partev** (c. 350-439): catholicos; with
  Mashtots translated the Bible (Syriac, then Greek,
  435); canons, prayers (?).
- **Koriun** (5th c.): *Վարք Մաշտոցի* (Life of
  Mashtots). **[lib]** `koriun`
- **Agathangelos** (5th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց*
  (History of the Armenians; the conversion), with
  the *Teaching of Saint Gregory*. **[lib]**
  `agathangelos`
- **Pawstos Buzand** (5th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց*
  (Epic Histories, the 4th century). **[lib]**
  `pavstos-buzand`
- **Ghazar Parpetsi** (c. 442-c. 500): *Պատմութիւն
  Հայոց* (History, 388-485) and *Թուղթ* to Vahan
  Mamikonian. **[lib]** `ghazar-parpetsi`
- **Yeghishe** (5th c.): *Վասն Վարդանայ եւ
  Հայոց պատերազմին* (the war of 451), *Խրատք*,
  *Կանոնք*, *Մեկնութիւն Արարածոց*. **[lib]**
  `yeghishe`
- **Movses Khorenatsi** (5th c.? possibly 7th-8th):
  *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (from Hayk to 440), *Geography*
  and rhetorical works (?). **[lib]**
  `movses-khorenatsi`
- **Eznik Koghbatsi** (c. 380-450): *Եղծ աղանդոց*
  (Refutation of the sects). **[lib]**
  `eznik-koghbatsi`
- **Hovhan Mandakuni** (c. 430-490): sermons and
  canons (some attributed). **[lib]**
  `hovhan-mandakuni`
- **Giwt Arahezatsi** (catholicos 461-478): *Թուղթ
  առ Վաչէ*. **[lib]** `giwt-arahezatsi`
- **Gregory the Illuminator** (c. 257-331): the
  *Յաճախապատում ճառք* (Teachings) and canons are
  attributed to him, written later. **[lib]**
  `grigor-lusavorich`
- **Translators** of the Golden Age: Hovsep
  Paghnatsi, Ghevond, Eznik, Koriun, and the pupils
  sent to Edessa, Constantinople and Alexandria.
  They gave Armenian the Bible, Eusebius' *Chronicle*
  and *Church History*, the Greek Fathers
  (Chrysostom, Basil, Athanasius, Cyril), and
  Ps.-Callisthenes' *Alexander Romance*.

## 6th century

- **Davit Anyaght** (David the Invincible, c. 470-
  c. 540): *Սահմանք իմաստասիրութեան* (Definitions of
  Philosophy), commentaries on Porphyry and on
  Aristotle's *Categories* and *Analytics*. **[lib]**
  `davit-anyaght`
- **Mambre Vertsanogh** (6th c.): sermons (*Ճառք*).
  **[lib]** `mambre-vertsanogh`
- **Ukhtanes** (10th c.? see below).
- **Hellenizing school** (6th-8th c.): very literal
  Armenian versions of Greek texts: Dionysius
  Thrax's *Grammar*, Philo, Plato, Aristotle's
  works, Porphyry, Nemesius, Zeno.
- **Khosrovik Targmanich** (7th c.?): works on
  Chrysostom's text and the church hymns.
  **[lib]** `khosrovik-targmanich`
- **Council of Dvin 506** and the *Book of Letters*
  (*Գիրք թղթոց*, collected 7th c.): letters of
  catholicoi, an important anonymous collection.

## 7th century

- **Anania Shirakatsi** (c. 610-685): arithmetic
  (*Հարցմանց եւ լուծմանց*), calendar (*Տոմար*),
  cosmography, *Աշխարհացոյց* (Geography, attributed),
  weights and measures, a *Chronicle* and an
  autobiography (*Յիշատակարան*). **[lib]**
  `anania-shirakatsi`
- **Sebeos** (7th c.): *Պատմութիւն* (the history of
  Heraclius, to 661). **[lib]** `sebeos`
- **Anonymous chronicle of 684** (the so-called
  *Anonymous Storyteller*, Pseudo-Sebeos, 7th c.).
  **[lib]** `anonymous-684`
- **Komitas Aghtsetsi** (catholicos 615-628): the
  hymn on St. Hripsime, church building; *Կնիք
  հաւատոյ* is a later compilation under other names.
  **[lib]** `komitas-aghtsetsi`
- **Hovhan Mamikonian** (7th c.? or later):
  *Պատմութիւն Տարօնոյ* (History of Taron; the
  attribution to Zenob Glak is disputed).
  **[lib]** `hovhan-mamikonean`
- **Stepanos Siunetsi** (c. 680-735): commentaries,
  hymns (*sharakans*), a translation of Greek works.
- **Vrtanes Kertogh** (c. 650-?): *Յաղագս
  պատկերամարտից* (against Iconoclasm) and letters.
  **[lib]** `vrtanes-kertogh`

## 8th century

- **Hovhan Ozhnetsi** (Hovhan Imastaser,
  c. 650-729): canons, *Against the Paulicians*,
  sermons. **[lib]** `hovhan-ozhnetsi`
- **Ghevond** (late 8th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց*
  (the Arab wars, 632-788). **[lib]** `ghevond`
- **Sahak Dzoraporetsi** (catholicos 677-703):
  canons, letters.
- **Mashtots Yeretsvan**? and the **Maštoc'**
  ritual: later compilations.

## 9th century

- **Tovma Artsruni** (c. 850-c. 904): *Պատմութիւն տանն
  Արծրունեաց* (the house of Artsruni; to 904).
  **[lib]** `tovma-artsruni`
- **Samuel Kamrjadzoretsi** (9th-10th): on faith.
- **Abraham Aghbatanetsi** (9th?): *Կանոն* and
  *Խոստովանութիւն*. **[lib]** `abraham-aghbatanetsi`
- **Anonymous Shapuh Bagratuni** (?): see below.

## 10th century

- **Hovhannes Drasxanakertsi** (c. 840-929):
  *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (History, to 923).
  **[lib]** `hovhannes-drasxanakertsi`
- **Movses Daskhurantsi** (Kaghankatvatsi, 10th c.):
  *Պատմութիւն Աղուանից աշխարհի* (History of the
  Caucasian Albanians). **[lib]**
  `movses-daskhurantsi`
- **Ukhtanes** (10th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց*
  (History: the Armenian church, the Georgian
  schism). **[lib]** `ukhtanes`
- **Grigor Narekatsi** (c. 951-1003): *Մատեան
  ողբերգութեան* (Book of Lamentation), *Գանձեր
  եւ Տաղեր*, commentary on the Song of Songs.
  **[lib]** `grigor-narekatsi`
- **Anania Narekatsi** (abbot, 10th c.): *Against the
  Tondrakians* and sermons.
- **Stepanos Taronetsi (Asoghik)** (c. 960-1019):
  *Տիեզերական պատմութիւն* (Universal History, to 1004).
  Not yet in the library.
- **Hovsep Gandzaketsi**, **Khachik**: hymns.
- **Sahak Vardapet** (Mrut): *Բացայայտութիւն*
  and answers to Photius and Ashot. **[lib]**
  `sahak-vardapet-mrut`
- **Seberianos Yemesatsi** (translated Greek
  homilies; 5th c. Syrian). **[lib]**
  `seberianos-yemesatsi`

## 11th century

- **Aristakes Lastivertsi** (c. 1000-1080):
  *Պատմութիւն* (the Seljuk conquest, 1000-1071).
  **[lib]** `aristakes-lastivertsi`
- **Grigor Magistros Pahlavuni** (c. 990-1058):
  *Թղթեր* (Letters), a poem of the Bible (1000
  lines), translations of Plato (*Timaeus*, *Phaedo*)
  and Euclid. **[lib]** `grigor-magistros`
- **Hovhan Sarkavag** (c. 1045-1129): sermons, a
  commentary on the hours and the *Chronicle* (?).
- **Anastas Vardapet**: *Վասն վանորէից ի
  Երուսաղէմ* (on the monasteries of Jerusalem;
  attribution ?). **[lib]** `anastas-vardapet`
- **Hovhannes Kozern** (c. 1100-1175?): *Ճառք*.
- **Grigoris Arsharuni** (11th-12th): *Մեկնութիւն
  ընթերցուածոց*. **[lib]** `grigoris-arsharuni`
- **Hakob Hrimetsi** (Yakob Ghrimetsi, c. 1090-?):
  tomar (calendar) works. **[lib]** `yakob-hrimetsi`
- **Grigor Tgha** (1133-1193): letters (*Նամականի*).
  **[lib]** `grigor-tgha`

## 12th century

- **Matthew of Edessa** (Matteos Urhayetsi,
  c. 1070-1144): *Ժամանակագրութիւն* (952-1136).
  **[lib]** `matthew-urhayetsi`
- **Nerses Shnorhali** (1102-1173): *Թուղթ
  ընդհանրական*, *Ողբ Եդեսիոյ*, *Վիպասանութիւն*,
  songs and prayers, riddles. **[lib]**
  `nerses-shnorhali`
- **Samuel Anetsi** (c. 1100-after 1180):
  *Հաւաքմունք* (Collections; chronicle).
  **[lib]** `samuel-anetsi`
- **Mkhitar Gosh** (c. 1120-1213): *Դատաստանագիրք
  Հայոց* (Lawcode, 1184), *Առակք* (Fables).
  **[lib]** `mkhitar-gosh`
- **Mkhitar Heratsi** (c. 1120-1200): *Ջերմանց
  մխիթարութիւն* (Consolation in Fevers, a medical
  book). **[lib]** `mkhitar-heratsi`
- **Mkhitar Anetsi** (12th c.): *Աշխարհավէպ
  հանդիսարանաց* (chronicle to 1163). **[lib]**
  `mkhitar-anetsi`
- **Nerses Lambronatsi** (1153-1198): commentaries
  on the liturgy (*Խորհրդածութիւնք*), letters,
  translations from Latin. **[lib]**
  `nerses-lambronatsi`
- **Michael the Syrian** (1126-1199): wrote in Syriac,
  with Armenian versions. **Hovhannes Imastaser**:
  see above.

## 13th century

- **Kirakos Gandzaketsi** (c. 1200-1271):
  *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (to 1265, with the Mongols).
  **[lib]** `kirakos-gandzaketsi`
- **Vardan Areveltsi** (c. 1200-1271): *Պատմութիւն*
  (World History), *Աշխարհացոյց*, commentaries on the
  Psalms, Grammar. **[lib]** `vardan-areveltsi`
- **Vardan Aygektsi** (c. 1170-1250): *Աղուէսագիրք*
  (*Ժողովածոյ առակաց*, fables). **[lib]**
  `vardan-aygektsi`
- **Kirakos Rshtuntsi** (13th c.):
  *Ժամանակագրութիւն*. **[lib]**
  `kirakos-rshtuntsi`
- **Smbat Sparapet** (1208-1276): *Դատաստանագիրք*
  (law, translated from Syriac) and *Տարեգիրք*
  (Chronicle, 951-1272). **[lib]** `smbat-sparapet`
- **Hetum Patmich** (c. 1235-1314): *Ժամանակագրութիւն*
  and genealogical works; *La Flor des estoires*
  (1307, French). **[lib]** `hetum-patmich`
- **Stepanos Orbelian** (c. 1250-1305): *Պատմութիւն
  նահանգին Սիսական* (Syunik). Not yet in the library.
- **Mkhitar Ayrivanetsi** (c. 1222-c. 1300):
  *Ժամանակագրութիւն* (to 1289). Not yet.
- **Hovhannes Erznkatsi** (c. 1230-1293): sermons,
  grammar, poems (*Բանք*). Not yet.
- **Vahram Rabuni** (13th c.): *Rhymed Chronicle*
  (Cilician kings). Not yet.
- **Hovhan Vanakan** (c. 1181-1251): history and
  teaching; his *History* is lost.
- **Mkhitar Skevratsi** (d. c. 1250): hymns.
- **Konstantin Erzinkatsi** (c. 1250-1310): hymns.
- **Kostandin Barsewn**: verse.
- **Hakob Klayetsi**: see below.

## 14th century (to 1400)

- **Grigor Tatevatsi** (1346-1409): *Գիրք հարցմանց*,
  *Ձմեռան հատոր*, *Ամառան հատոր*. **[lib]**
  `grigor-tatevatsi` (died 1409: borderline).
- **Hovhan Vorotnetsi** (1315-1386): sermons,
  commentary on the Gospels, teacher of Tatevatsi.
- **Esayi Nchetsi** (c. 1260-1338): commentaries on
  Isaiah and others.
- **Hovhannes Tsortsoretsi** (1270-1325): sermons.
- **Nerses Mshetsi** (1300?-?) and **Mkhitar Aparanetsi**
  (14th c.): theology.
- **Hakob Klayetsi** (14th c.): *Chronicle*.
- **Hovhannes Pluz Yerznkatsi** (1230-1293): see 13th.
- **Nerses Paliants**: 17th (out of range, **[lib]**
  `nerses-paliants`). **Zakaria Kanakertsi**,
  **Arakel Davrizhetsi** and **Grigor Daranaghtsi**:
  17th c.; **Tovma Metsopetsi** (c. 1378-1446):
  15th c. **[lib]** (see their folders).

## Anonymous and collective works

- The **Bible** (Mashtots, 5th c.; the 1895 edition).
  **[lib]** `bible`
- The *Book of Letters*, *Book of Canons*
  (*Կանոնագիրք Հայոց*), the *Ճառընտիր*
  (collections of sermons), the *Ժամագիրք*
  (Book of Hours), the *Գանձարան*, the *Տոմար*.
- **Colophons** (*յիշատակարանք*): scribal notes in
  manuscripts from the 5th century on (published in
  collections for the 5th-12th c., 13th, 14th).
- Lives of saints and martyrs (*Վարք*,
  *Վկայաբանութիւն*): St. Hripsime, Gayane,
  Shushanik (Jacob Tsurtaveli, Georgian), Vardan.
- The *Alexander Romance*, *Physiologus*, and
  *Barlaam and Josaphat* in Armenian.
- The *Armenian Geography* and the *Tomar of Anania*.

## What this list does not cover

- Georgian and Albanian works by Armenians, and the
  Armenian authors writing in Greek, Latin, French or
  Persian (Hetum's French; Hovhannes Pluz; Hayton).
- Everything after 1400, and manuscripts without an
  author.
- The authors above that are marked "Not yet" in the
  library (Asoghik, Orbelian, Ayrivanetsi,
  Erznkatsi, Vahram Rabuni) are the first to fetch
  next from Digilib (`notes/download-priority.md`).
