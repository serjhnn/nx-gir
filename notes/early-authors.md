# Armenian authors before the 15th century

Authors known by name (and the main anonymous works)
from the 5th century to 1400, with their books.

**Status: NOT checked against Matenagirk Hayots.**
`matenagirq.am` gives a proxy 502 (even when allowed)
and `matenadaran.am` a 403, and a web search found no
volume-by-volume list. Instead the list was compared
with the Digilib catalogue (`notes/digilib.tsv`,
section "Հայ դասական մատենագրութիւն", 445 author
names): authors and titles marked **[digilib]** are
there. Dates are still from general knowledge; the
book titles of authors we hold are from
`books/LIBRARY.tsv`.
Dates are the usual ones, many approximate; "?" marks
a doubtful date or attribution. **[lib]** means the
author is in `books/grabar/` (folder name given).

Only Armenian authors are listed; texts known to be
translated from Greek or Syriac appear only as
translations.

## 5th century

- **Mesrop Mashtots** (c. 362-440): the alphabet
  (405); letters and prayers (?). Life: Koriun.
- **Sahak Partev** (c. 350-439): catholicos; with
  Mashtots translated the Bible; canons, prayers (?).
- **Koriun** (5th c.): *Վարք Մաշտոցի* (Life of
  Mashtots). **[lib]** `koriun`
- **Agathangelos** (5th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց*
  (the conversion under Trdat) with the *Teaching of
  Saint Gregory*. **[lib]** `agathangelos`
- **Pawstos Buzand** (5th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց*
  (Epic Histories, the 4th century). **[lib]**
  `pavstos-buzand`
- **Ghazar Parpetsi** (c. 442-c. 500): *Պատմութիւն
  Հայոց* (388-485) and a letter to Vahan Mamikonian.
  **[lib]** `ghazar-parpetsi`
- **Yeghishe** (5th c.): *Վասն Վարդանայ եւ Հայոց
  պատերազմին* (the war of 451), *Խրատք*, *Կանոնք*,
  *Մեկնութիւն Արարածոց*. **[lib]** `yeghishe`
- **Movses Khorenatsi** (5th c.; some date him to the
  7th-8th): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (Hayk to 440); the
  *Geography* and rhetorical works are attributed.
  **[lib]** `movses-khorenatsi`
- **Eznik Koghbatsi** (c. 380-450): *Եղծ աղանդոց*
  (Refutation of the sects). **[lib]**
  `eznik-koghbatsi`
- **Hovhan Mandakuni** (c. 430-490): sermons and
  canons (some attributed). **[lib]**
  `hovhan-mandakuni`
- **Giwt Arahezatsi** (catholicos 461-478): *Թուղթ
  առ Վաչէ*. **[lib]** `giwt-arahezatsi`
- **Vachagan III** (king of Caucasian Albania,
  487-510): *Սահմանադրութիւն կանոնական* (the canons of
  the Aghuen council, 488). **[digilib]**
- **Mambre Vertsanogh** (5th-6th c.): sermons
  (*Ճառք*). **[lib]** `mambre-vertsanogh`
- **Gregory the Illuminator** (c. 257-331): the
  *Յաճախապատում ճառք* and canons carry his name but
  were written later. **[lib]** `grigor-lusavorich`
- **Seberianos Yemesatsi** (Severian of Emesa,
  c. 400): a Greek writer whose sermons survive in
  Armenian; not an Armenian author. **[lib]**
  `seberianos-yemesatsi`
- The **translators** of the Golden Age (Hovsep
  Paghnatsi and others) gave Armenian the Bible,
  Eusebius' *Chronicle*, the Greek Fathers and the
  *Alexander Romance*.

## 5th-6th century

- **Davit Anyaght** (David the Invincible, c. 470-
  c. 540): *Սահմանք իմաստասիրութեան* (Definitions of
  Philosophy) and commentaries on Porphyry and
  Aristotle's *Categories* and *Analytics*. **[lib]**
  `davit-anyaght`
- **Hellenizing school** (6th-8th c.): literal Armenian
  versions of Greek texts (Dionysius Thrax's
  *Grammar*, Philo, Aristotle, Plato).
- **Nerses II Bagrevandatsi** (catholicos c. 548-557):
  letters (*Թղթեր*), the covenant of the Armenian
  church (*Ուխտ միաբանութեան*), canons. **[digilib]**
- **Hovhannes Gabeghenatsi** (6th-7th c.?): letters
  and *Ի յայտնութիւն Քրիստոսի*. **[digilib]**
- **Mathusagha**, bishop of Syunik (date ?): a letter.
  **[digilib]**
- **Anonymous works of the 6th c.:** the martyrdoms
  of Shushanik and of Thaddeus, the *Life of
  Vachagan*. **[digilib]**
- The *Book of Letters* (*Գիրք թղթոց*, letters of
  catholicoi, from the Council of Dvin 506 on;
  compiled in the 7th c.), anonymous.

## 7th century

- **Abraham I Aghbatanetsi** (catholicos 607-615):
  *Կանոն* and *Խոստովանութիւն*. **[lib]**
  `abraham-aghbatanetsi`
- **Komitas Aghtsetsi** (catholicos 615-628): the hymn
  on St. Hripsime, the church of Hripsime; *Կնիք
  հաւատոյ* (Seal of Faith) is a compilation linked
  to his time (?). **[lib]** `komitas-aghtsetsi`
- **Anania Shirakatsi** (c. 610-685): arithmetic,
  calendar (*Տոմար*), cosmography, *Աշխարհացոյց*
  (attributed), weights and measures, an
  autobiography. **[lib]** `anania-shirakatsi`
- **Hovhan Mayragometsi** (7th c.): *Վերլուծութիւն
  կաթողիկէ եկեղեցւոյ* and letters. **[digilib]**
- **Philon Tirakatsi** (7th c.?): *Ժամանակագրութիւն*.
  **[digilib]**
- **Sebeos** (7th c.): *Պատմութիւն* (to 661). **[lib]**
  `sebeos`
- **Anonymous chronicle of 684** (Pseudo-Sebeos).
  **[lib]** `anonymous-684`
- **Vrtanes Kertogh** (7th-8th c.): *Յաղագս
  պատկերամարտից* (on images) and letters. **[lib]**
  `vrtanes-kertogh`
- **Stepanos Siunetsi** (c. 680-735): commentaries,
  hymns (*sharakans*).
- **Anastas Vardapet** (7th-8th c.?): *Վասն վանորէից
  ի Երուսաղէմ*. **[lib]** `anastas-vardapet`
- **Anania Targmanich** and **Khosrovik Targmanich**
  (translators, dates ?): **[lib]** `anania-targmanich`,
  `khosrovik-targmanich`
- **Hovhan Mamikonian** (*Պատմութիւն Տարօնոյ*, History
  of Taron; written 7th c.? attributed to Zenob
  Glak; later redaction). **[lib]**
  `hovhan-mamikonean`

## 8th century

- **Hovhan Ozhnetsi** (Hovhan Imastaser,
  c. 650-729): canons, *Against the Paulicians*,
  sermons. **[lib]** `hovhan-ozhnetsi`
- **Ghevond** (late 8th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (the
  Arab wars, 632-788). **[lib]** `ghevond`
- **Sahak Dzoraporetsi** (catholicos 677-703): canons
  and *Ճառ յարմաւենեացն օր*. **[digilib]**
- **Sahak Ughkatsi** (date ?): *Կանոնք ժողովոյն
  Կարնոյ*. **[digilib]**

## 9th century

- **Tovma Artsruni** (c. 850-c. 904): *Պատմութիւն տանն
  Արծրունեաց* (to 904). **[lib]** `tovma-artsruni`
- **Samuel Kamrjadzoretsi** (9th-10th c.?): works on
  the faith.
- **Smbat Bagratuni** (date ?): letters (*Թղթեր*).
  **[digilib]**

## 10th century

- **Hovhannes Drasxanakertsi** (c. 840-929):
  *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (to 923). **[lib]**
  `hovhannes-drasxanakertsi`
- **Movses Daskhurantsi** (Kaghankatvatsi, 10th c.):
  *Պատմութիւն Աղուանից աշխարհի* (Caucasian Albania).
  **[lib]** `movses-daskhurantsi`
- **Ukhtanes** (10th c.): *Պատմութիւն Հայոց* (the
  Armenian church and the schism with Georgia).
  **[lib]** `ukhtanes`
- **Grigor Narekatsi** (c. 951-1003): *Մատեան
  ողբերգութեան*, *Գանձեր եւ Տաղեր*, commentary on
  the Song of Songs. **[lib]** `grigor-narekatsi`
- **Anania Narekatsi** (10th c.): *Against the
  Tondrakians* and sermons.
- **Stepanos Taronetsi (Asoghik)** (c. 960-1019):
  *Տիեզերական պատմութիւն* (to 1004). Not in the
  library yet.

## 11th century

- **Aristakes Lastivertsi** (c. 1000-1080):
  *Պատմութիւն* (1000-1071). **[lib]**
  `aristakes-lastivertsi`
- **Grigor Magistros Pahlavuni** (c. 990-1058):
  *Թղթեր*, a verse retelling of the Bible, versions
  of Plato and Euclid. **[lib]** `grigor-magistros`
- **Petros Syunetsi** (11th c.?): panegyrics and
  sermons. **[digilib]**
- **Hovhan Sarkavag** (c. 1045-1129): sermons and a
  commentary on the hours.
- **Grigoris Arsharuni** (11th-12th c.): *Մեկնութիւն
  ընթերցուածոց*. **[lib]** `grigoris-arsharuni`

## 12th century

- **Matthew of Edessa** (c. 1070-1144):
  *Ժամանակագրութիւն* (952-1136). **[lib]**
  `matthew-urhayetsi`
- **Nerses Shnorhali** (1102-1173): *Թուղթ
  ընդհանրական*, *Ողբ Եդեսիոյ*, *Վիպասանութիւն*,
  songs, prayers, riddles. **[lib]** `nerses-shnorhali`
- **Samuel Anetsi** (c. 1100-after 1180): *Հաւաքմունք*
  (chronicle). **[lib]** `samuel-anetsi`
- **Mkhitar Gosh** (c. 1120-1213): *Դատաստանագիրք
  Հայոց* (1184) and *Առակք* (Fables). **[lib]**
  `mkhitar-gosh`
- **Mkhitar Heratsi** (c. 1120-1200): *Ջերմանց
  մխիթարութիւն* (medicine). **[lib]**
  `mkhitar-heratsi`
- **Mkhitar Anetsi** (12th c.): *Աշխարհավէպ
  հանդիսարանաց* (chronicle to 1163). **[lib]**
  `mkhitar-anetsi`
- **Grigor Tgha** (1133-1193): letters (*Նամականի*).
  **[lib]** `grigor-tgha`
- **Nerses Lambronatsi** (1153-1198): *Խորհրդածութիւնք
  ի կարգս եկեղեցւոյ*, letters, translations from
  Latin. **[lib]** `nerses-lambronatsi`
- **Sahak Mrut** (Vardapet, date ?): *Բացայայտութիւն*,
  answers to Photius and to Ashot. **[lib]**
  `sahak-vardapet-mrut`

## Anonymous and collective works

- The **Bible** (5th c.; our text is the 1895
  edition). **[lib]** `bible`
- The *Book of Letters*, canons (*Կանոնագիրք Հայոց*),
  the *Ճառընտիր* (sermon collections), the *Ժամագիրք*.
- **Colophons** (*յիշատակարանք*): scribal notes in
  manuscripts from the 5th century on.
- Lives and martyrdoms of saints (Hripsime, Vardan).
- Armenian versions of the *Alexander Romance*.
- Anonymous chronicles (*Անանուն ժամանակագիր*,
  Digilib lists a dozen, Middle Armenian, mostly
  14th-17th c.) and anonymous *Տաղեր* (songs).
- The many Middle Armenian poets of the *Տաղեր*
  in Digilib (Hovhannes, Zakaria, Kirakos, Minas
  and others) are mostly 15th-17th c.; each needs a
  date before it is placed here.
