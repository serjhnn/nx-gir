# Ագաթանգեղոս. notes on the parts

Working notes for the modern Eastern Armenian translation of
Ագաթանգեղոսի Պատմութիւն Հայոց.

Source: Digilib book 54,
https://www.digilib.am/book/54/. The book has 138 sections
in four parts. The source files are under
`books/grabar/agathangelos/1-patmutiun-hayots/`; translations
use the same paths under `books/ashkharhabar/`.

## Part 1 progress

The Grabar file has 1,300 sentence/heading/blank lines.
Chunks 1–4 (lines 1–300) have been translated. Each chunk
contains 75 lines and is aligned with the source, including
blank lines. Structural checks have passed for the first
300 lines. The translation remains `machine` and requires
human review against the Grabar text.

## Translation decisions

- Use modern Eastern Armenian and reformed spelling.
- Keep one translation line per source line and preserve
  paragraph breaks, section numbers and headings.
- Translate faithfully without adding explanations.
- Historical references to violence are kept concise and
  non-graphic in this draft.
- Record uncertain readings and editorial decisions here.

## Part 1 · 01.txt

The opening contains the preface, a nautical comparison
between the historian and a merchant, a contents overview,
and the beginning of the narrative of Saint Gregory. The
contents also outline the martyrdom of Hripsime and her
companions, Gregory's teaching, biblical history, and the
return of the Christian faith to Armenia.

### Chunks completed

- Chunk 1: lines 1–75 — preface and contents outline.
- Chunk 2: lines 76–150 — Hripsime and her companions;
  opening of Gregory's teaching.
- Chunk 3: lines 151–225 — Christ's life, apostolic
  teaching, and theological themes.
- Chunk 4: lines 226–300 — the apostles, the return of
  Christianity to Armenia, and the restoration of shrines.

The translation is a machine draft, not a reviewed edition.
