# Ագաթանգեղոս. notes on the parts

Working notes for the modern Eastern Armenian translation of
Ագաթանգեղոսի Պատմութիւն Հայոց.

Source: Digilib book 54,
https://www.digilib.am/book/54/. The book has 138 sections
in four parts. The source files are under
`books/grabar/agathangelos/1-patmutiun-hayots/`; translations
use the same paths under `books/ashkharhabar/`.

## How to do the next part

Part 1 is in progress. Its Grabar file was split into
1300 sentence/heading/blank lines. Chunk 1 (lines 1–75)
is translated. Continue with lines 76–150, then in
75-line chunks, checking line count, blank-line positions,
locked headings, spelling and punctuation after every chunk.
Keep the translation status `machine` until a person reviews
the whole part beside the Grabar.

## Part 1 · 01.txt

The opening contains the preface, a nautical comparison
between the historian and a merchant, a contents overview,
and the beginning of the narrative of Saint Gregory. The
contents also outline the martyrdom of Hripsime and her
companions and Gregory's teaching.

### Chunk 1 · lines 1–75

- Lines 1–5: part title, preface heading and separator.
- Lines 7–13: the preface's extended nautical image. Its
  syntax and some metaphors are difficult; review against
  the Grabar.
- Lines 15–41: contents and the preface's summary.
- Lines 43–75: contents of the life of Gregory and the
  opening events of the narrative.
- Lines 55–71: the torture episodes are rendered in
  restrained, non-graphic language in this draft; a human
  reviewer should compare the wording with the source.

Unclear readings and choices should be added here with
their line numbers as later chunks are translated.
