nxGir (Նախագիր): an offline copy of nxgir.am
=============================================

Open it with Python 3 (Linux, macOS, Windows, Termux):

    python3 serve.py

then open http://localhost:8000/ in a browser (it
opens by itself where there is one). Stop with
Ctrl+C. Your places, bookmarks and notes stay in
the browser, on this device.

Opening index.html by double click does not work:
browsers do not let a page read its data files from
disk.

Այս պանակը nxgir.am-ի օֆլայն օրինակն է: բացեք
տերմինալում՝ python3 serve.py, հետո դիտարկչում՝
http://localhost:8000/
