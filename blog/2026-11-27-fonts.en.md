# How we made two fonts for Grabar

theme: tech

Old Armenian books are not read in a font like any other. We wanted the text in nxGir to look as if it were written by a scribe, so we made two fonts of our own: nxGir Dzeragir, from the hand of a real manuscript, and nxGir Bolorgir, a heavy letter written with a broad pen. This short video shows how.

![How the fonts of nxGir were made](youtube:fQMG8aamcdY fonts-video.jpg)

## Dzeragir: a scribe's hand

The letters come from a Gospel book of the 17th century, Walters Ms. W.541, whose page images are free to use (CC0). First we cut the black ink of every page into pieces, about 6,300 of them, and grouped the pieces by shape. Then came the clever part: we lined every page up with the printed text of the Grabar Gospels (1895). Where a line of the manuscript matched the text, every piece learned which letter it was. 460 lines were found on 27 pages.

For each letter we laid its best copies over each other and kept the ink that most of them share. That gave 27 small letters, whole. The scribe joins the other letters to their neighbours, so those were cut out at the thinnest place of the joining stroke, checked by eye and averaged too. His capitals are the red initials that open his sentences. Every letter in the font is his; only a few marks he never wrote (some punctuation, the digits) are drawn with a round pen of his weight and slant, and Latin letters come from the next font in the list. A thinner version, Dzeragir Thin, comes from the same run.

## Bolorgir: a broad pen

The second font starts from a printed one, Noto Serif Armenian. Each letter is drawn big, made wider and lower, thinned to its middle line, and written again with a broad pen held at 35 degrees, as in a bolorgir manuscript. The picture is then traced back to curves. The result is heavy and calm, easy to read on a phone.

## Use them

Both fonts are free (SIL OFL 1.1), like the Noto font they come from or the manuscript they were taken from. Open the text settings of the reader and pick one from the list of fonts; they change only the text of the books. The scripts that make them are in the repository (`scripts/render-font.py`, `scripts/render-scan-font.py`), and the letters of the scribe are in `sources/w541/`.
