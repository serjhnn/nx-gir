# Welcome to the nxGir blog

Welcome! The nxGir website opened on 7 November 2026. Here we will write about what is new in nxGir and about the idea behind it.

## The idea

Many old Armenian books were written in Grabar, classical Armenian. Today they are hard to read, even for people who speak Armenian well: the words, the forms and the spelling have changed over the centuries, and many of these texts have never been translated.

nxGir wants to make reading these old texts easier with the help of AI translations. Each part of a book is translated into modern Armenian, and the translation stands next to the Grabar original, sentence by sentence, line by line. Tap any word to see its meaning, its dictionary form and where else it appears in the book.

The translations are made by a machine and are not yet checked by a specialist, so read them as a help to the original, not in place of it. If you find a mistake, please write to us.

## What comes next

In this blog we will tell about new books on the shelf, newly translated parts, and how the reader and the dictionary grow. Thank you for reading with us.
