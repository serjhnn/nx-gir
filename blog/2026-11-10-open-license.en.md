# Our translations are free to share: help us make them better

theme: site

Today the modern Armenian translations of nxGir got a license: CC BY-SA 4.0, a Creative Commons license. We added it for one reason above all: so that people can join the work and know where their work goes.

## Why a license

Until now the translations were ours to read, but nobody knew what they were allowed to do with them. Could a teacher print a sermon for a class? Could someone correct a part and publish it on their own site? Without a license the honest answer was "ask first", and few people do.

A clear license also matters to anyone who helps. If you spend evenings correcting a translation, you want to know that your work will stay open for everyone and will not be closed off by someone else later.

## What CC BY-SA allows

Anyone may copy, share and change the translations, for any purpose, on two conditions. They name the source, Նախագիր (nxgir.am), and the license. And they share their own version under the same license, so that it stays free too.

The Grabar texts themselves are centuries old and belong to everyone; the license covers our translations only.

## Machine first, people next

The translations were first made by a machine, by Claude, an AI model. A machine draft is a good start, but it needs people who know Grabar to read it beside the original and correct it. That review is now under way.

Above every translated part the reader now shows where it stands: still a machine draft, being reviewed, or reviewed, and by whom. Your name stands there when you review a part.

## How to help

If you read Grabar, or simply notice a mistake, you can help. Open a translated part, press ⇆ Parallel and read it beside the original. The steps, and the license of contributions, are in the file CONTRIBUTING.md of the project on GitHub: github.com/serjhnn/nx-gir. Or write to us; every correction counts.

Thank you for reading, and welcome to the work.
