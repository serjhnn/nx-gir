# Features

What Նախագիր can do today. Everything works in the browser, on a phone or a computer, with no account.

## Grabar and modern Armenian side by side

Open a translated part and press ⇆ Parallel: the Grabar original and its modern Armenian translation are shown line by line, each line next to its pair. On a wide screen the two can stand in columns or each line under its pair.

## Free translations, reviewed by people

Our modern Armenian translations are under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license: copy, share and change them, naming Նախագիր (nxgir.am) and keeping the same license. They were first made by machine (Claude); above each one the reader shows whether it is still a draft, being reviewed or reviewed, and by whom. Anyone can help review them.

## Word lookup

Tap any word to see what it means, in English or in Armenian. The meanings come from Wiktionary; where it has nothing, a meaning written by AI is shown and marked as such.

## Scribal abbreviations

Old manuscripts and books shorten common words (վրդ for վարդապետ). Tap one and its full form is shown, with the source it comes from.

## Every place a word appears

The word you tapped is marked everywhere in the part. The arrows ‹ › take you from one place to the next; on a keyboard, n and N do the same.

## The sentence of a word

Tapping a word also highlights the whole sentence it belongs to, so long Grabar sentences are easier to follow.

## Where you stopped

The site remembers where you stopped reading each book, in this browser. The home page shows what you read last under Continue reading, with how far you got; open a book and it starts where you left it. All your readings are on the Readings page.

## Contents

☰ Contents lists the chapters and sermons of the part you are reading and marks the one you are in. Tap one to go there; tap ☰ again to close the list.

## From one part to the next

At the end of each part of a long book, two cards lead to the part before and the part after it, with the chapters each one begins with, and a link to all the parts of the book. At the bottom of every page are the main pages of the site, the license of the translations and how to help.

## Text settings

Make the text bigger or smaller, set it on the left or in the center, change the line spacing, put each sentence on its own line, read in a column or across the whole width, and choose the font. On a phone these are behind the settings button. The site remembers your choice.

## Light, dark and black

The theme button switches between a light page, a dark one and a black one for reading at night.

## How far you have read

A bar above the text shows how much of the part you have read.

## The Library

The Library page lists the Grabar books by author, with how many parts each has and how much of it is translated.

## Blog, Timeline and Statistics

The Blog tells what is new. The Timeline shows the main steps of the project, and the Statistics page how big the library and the site are.
