# Interpretations

Our own texts on the books in `books/`: readings,
reflections and commentary, in Russian (`ru/`) and
English (`en/`). They are content, not code, so the
60-character line limit does not apply.

One text per file, named by the passage it is about
and a short title in Latin letters, e.g.
`ps-118-33-37-pustota-i-put.md`. The first line is the
title, with the passage in brackets.
