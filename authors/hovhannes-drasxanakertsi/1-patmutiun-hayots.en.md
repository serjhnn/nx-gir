# History of the Armenians

written: around 924
kind: a history of Armenia to the 920s
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The history of Armenia written by a catholicos: from the beginnings, briefly, to his own time, which he tells at length as a witness and a player, the Bagratid kingdom under Smbat I and Ashot II the Iron, the wars with the Sajid emirs, the martyrdom of Smbat, and his own embassies and flight to Vaspurakan.

Here it is in Digilib's text, three parts, opening with the author's preface (Յառաջաբան պատմութեան).
