# Works on the Calendar

written: early 15th century
kind: works on the calendar
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Yakob's teaching on the calendar, much of it in questions and answers: how many cycles of the sun and the moon there are, that the sun's cycle turns in 28 years and the moon's in 19, how the great cycle is completed, how to find the year and the new year by the rules of the old textbook. He drew also on his own watching of the phases of the moon.

Here it is in Digilib's text, three parts, in [1], [2] sections with no headings.
