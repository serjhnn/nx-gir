# Milestones and Air Measures

written: 7th century
kind: distances on earth and in the sky
source: the book itself

Distances in stadia, paces, feet and miles: the stations from Dvin to Karin, Rome, Jerusalem and other cities, and then the distances up to the spheres of the planets.

The last paragraph is a dedication note to a reader, with corrupt names. Here it is in Digilib's text, with a translation into modern Armenian beside it, line for line (machine, not yet reviewed).
