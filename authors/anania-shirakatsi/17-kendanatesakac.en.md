# On the Zodiac

written: 7th century
kind: on the stars and the zodiac
source: the book itself

On the band of stars called the zodiac (the "kinds of animals"): the seven wandering stars and the days of the week named after them, the seven zones of the heavens, and the signs, written, the author says, at the request of "a beloved son" who asked him to set them out again.
