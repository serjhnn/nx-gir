# On Weights

written: 7th century
kind: a list of weights
source: the book itself

The weights of the old world and how they relate: the assarion, the lepton and the danka, "a weight of two grains of barley is called an assarion", and so on to the greater ones.

Here it is in Digilib's text, with a translation into modern Armenian beside it, line for line (machine, not yet reviewed).
