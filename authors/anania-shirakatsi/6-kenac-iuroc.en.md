# On the Course of My Life

written: 7th century
kind: an autobiography
source: Wikipedia · the book itself

"I, Anania Shirakatsi, from the village of Anania": the only life a classical Armenian author wrote of himself. He tells how he found no master of mathematics in Armenia, went to Trebizond, studied there for years under the scholar Tychicus, and came back to teach.
