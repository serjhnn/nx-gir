# Puzzles for Feasts

written: 7th century
kind: number puzzles
source: Wikipedia · the book itself

Nine number puzzles to play at a feast: "Say to your friend, I can tell what you are thinking", and then reckon the number of the hour, the wine, a purse, eggs, pomegranates or birds. The text is corrupt, and some steps cannot be followed.

Here it is in Digilib's text, with a translation into modern Armenian beside it, line for line (machine, not yet reviewed).
