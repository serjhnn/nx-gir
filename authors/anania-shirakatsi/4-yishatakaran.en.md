# Colophon

written: 7th century
kind: a colophon
source: the book itself

A colophon of five lines, damaged in the source: Anania writes that he sought this knowledge with much labour, speaks of the reckoning of the cycles, and signs himself "I, Anania Shirakatsi, from the village of Anen" (the place is a guess).

Here it is in Digilib's text, with a translation into modern Armenian beside it, line for line (machine, not yet reviewed).
