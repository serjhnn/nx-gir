# On the Theophany

written: 7th century
kind: a homily on the feast of the Theophany
source: the book itself

A homily on the feast of the Lord's appearing. Anania argues that a feast of the Nativity twelve days before the feast of the Baptism was not set by the apostles or those after them, but came later, and defends the Armenian custom of keeping the Nativity and the Baptism on one day.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, with the manuscript's page marks.
