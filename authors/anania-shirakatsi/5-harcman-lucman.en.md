# On Questions and Solutions

written: 7th century
kind: arithmetic problems
source: the book itself

Problems in arithmetic, each a "question" told as a story, and its answer: how many soldiers there were before a battle with the Persians, as the author heard from his father of the brave Zavrak Kamsarakan; how much was in a treasury; how many had a merchant. It is one of the oldest books of arithmetic problems in Armenian.
