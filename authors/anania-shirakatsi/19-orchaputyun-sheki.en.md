# Reckoning of the Moon

written: 7th century
kind: rules for the moon's reckoning
source: the book itself

Rules for counting with the moon: how long it shines each night of its month, how it completes twelve or thirteen months in a year of 354 or 384 days, and how to find the leap year, each rule set as "if you wish to know …".

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, with the manuscript's page marks.
