# Easter Sermon

written: 7th century
kind: a sermon on the date of Easter
source: the book itself

A sermon on the feast of Easter: why the Lord set it by the moon, on the fourteenth day of the month, as He commanded Moses, and how its day is reckoned by the hours of the sun and the moon.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, with the manuscript's page marks (/20a/).
