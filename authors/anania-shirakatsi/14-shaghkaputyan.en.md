# On the Conjunction of the Sun and the Moon

written: 7th century
kind: a calculation of eclipses
source: the book itself

A recipe for calculating the meetings of the sun and the moon, their eclipses, with the numbers in Armenian letters. It is cut off at the end on Digilib.

Here it is in Digilib's text, with a translation into modern Armenian beside it, line for line (machine, not yet reviewed).
