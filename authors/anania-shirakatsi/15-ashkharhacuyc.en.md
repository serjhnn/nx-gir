# Geography

written: 7th century (ascribed)
kind: a geography of the world
source: Wikipedia · the book itself

The Ashkharhatsuyts, the first geography in Armenian. It goes through the climates of the world, each by a city or land it passes through, Syunik, Alexandria, Rhodes, Rome, Constantinople, Scythia, and describes the lands of Europe, Africa and Asia, Armenia among them at length.

It is ascribed to Anania; some have ascribed it to Movses Khorenatsi. Here it is in Digilib's text, with a translation into modern Armenian beside it, line for line (machine, not yet reviewed); each ordinal line ("Second · through Syunik") is an entry of the Contents.
