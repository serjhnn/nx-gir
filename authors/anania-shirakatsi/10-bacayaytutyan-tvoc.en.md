# On the Explanation of Numbers

written: 7th century
kind: on the meaning of numbers
source: the book itself

What numbers mean: first from the Scriptures and then from the learning of the pagans, beginning with the one, "for all things must have one beginning, as Plato says", and why we say "one" and not "first".

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, with the manuscript's page marks.
