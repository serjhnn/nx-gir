# History

written: 7th century
kind: a chronicle
source: the book itself

A chronicle of the world: from Adam and Enosh, "who first hoped to call on the name of God", and Noah, through the sons of Noah and the division of the lands, their islands and cities, and then by the count of the years, the judges and kings of Israel, the Ptolemies and the Roman emperors with their consulships.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one part, with the manuscript's page marks.
