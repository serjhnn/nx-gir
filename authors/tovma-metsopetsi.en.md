# Tovma Metsopetsi

years: 1378 – 1446
role: historian, vardapet
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

Born in the village of Aghi near Archesh, he studied at the monastery of Metsop. Fleeing the armies of Timur and the Turkmens, he taught at Sukhara, Tatev, Lim and Metsop. After Mashtots he is the first Armenian writer whose life was written by a pupil, Kirakos Banaser.

He fought Catholic influence in the Armenian Church and in 1441 helped bring the see of the catholicos back from Sis to Etchmiadzin. His main work, the History of Timur and his successors, is mostly an eyewitness's memories and an important source on Armenia and Georgia in the 14th and 15th centuries.
