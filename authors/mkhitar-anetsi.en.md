# Mkhitar Anetsi

years: 12th century
role: historian, senior priest of the Cathedral of Ani
source: Armenian Wikipedia

He was the senior priest of the Cathedral of Ani. At the request of the vardapet Grigor of Harichavank he wrote the Ashkharhavep (Matean ashkharhavep handisarants), which probably had three parts: from King Paruyr to the Bagratid King Gagik I, the events of the 11th and 12th centuries, and the Crusades.

Only the first chapters of the first part, up to the twenty-seventh, survive. His other works are lost; we know of them from Vardan Areveltsi and Stepanos Orbelian.
