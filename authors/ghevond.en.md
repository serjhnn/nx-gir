# Ghevond

years: 8th century
role: historian, vardapet
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

Little is known of his life: he was a vardapet and a learned monk. He wrote his history at the request of Shapuh Bagratuni, and from 774 onwards he tells of events as an eyewitness.

His History of the Armenians covers the years 632 to 789 and is the main source on Arab rule in Armenia; for 662–770 it is almost the only one. It tells of the revolts of 703, 748, 762 and 774–775. The text was long thought lost and was found only in the 1840s.
