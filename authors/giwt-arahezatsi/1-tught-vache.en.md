# Letter to Vache

written: 5th century
kind: a letter
source: the book itself

The letter of "Bishop Giwt to Saint Vache". Giwt praises Vache's virtue and speaks of the heroes of the faith before him, among them the brave Urnayr, who came with his nobles to Gregory the Illuminator.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, in numbered verses. Digilib marks this book "mixed": a modern foreword or notes stand beside the Grabar.
