# History

written: 7th century (the 650s – 660s)
kind: a history of the 6th and 7th centuries
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The main source for Armenian history in the 6th and 7th centuries: the wars of Byzantium and Persia, the Armenian princes between them, the fall of the Sasanians and the first Arab conquests. It is the oldest surviving detailed account of the rise of Islam, and one of the very few written outside it.

It survives in a manuscript written at Baghesh in 1672, with no author's name or title; the name Sebeos was given to it in the 19th century, only as a guess.
