# Mambre Vertsanogh

years: 5th century
role: writer, commentator, homilist
source: Armenian Wikipedia

He lived in the second half of the 5th century, and little is known of him. By tradition he was the brother of Movses Khorenatsi and one of the younger pupils of Sahak Partev and Mesrop Mashtots, and studied in Alexandria. Later writers called him "the great philosopher".

His homilies survive, on the raising of Lazarus and on Christ's entry into Jerusalem, with a fragment of a commentary on grammar. His language is the Armenian of the early Hellenizing school, and his interpretation is allegorical.
