# Zakaria Kanakertsi

years: 1627 – 1699
role: historian, deacon
source: Armenian Wikipedia

Born lame in Kanaker, at thirteen he joined the brotherhood of Hovhannavank and spent his whole life there, as scribe, deacon and then archdeacon. He counted Arakel Davrizhetsi as his teacher and helped him correct the Book of Histories.

He worked beside Catholicos Hakob IV Jughayetsi and on his errands went to Qazvin, Smyrna and Constantinople. His History tells of his own time and of the cruelty of Persian rule, which he saw himself: the khan's tax collector tortured his father to death.
