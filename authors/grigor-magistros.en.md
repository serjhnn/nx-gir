# Grigor Magistros

years: c. 990 – 1058
role: prince, scholar, translator
source: Wikipedia

A prince of the Pahlavuni family, son of Vasak Pahlavuni, lord of Bjni, he was educated in Ani. He stood by King Gagik II and the independence of the Bagratid kingdom; after Byzantium annexed it, he served the empire as duke of Mesopotamia and persecuted the Tondrakians.

He knew Greek and Syriac and gathered the Armenian manuscripts of scientific and philosophical worth, the works of Anania Shirakatsi among them. He translated Plato's Timaeus and Phaedo, which are lost. His Letters discuss politics and philosophy and are an important historical source.
