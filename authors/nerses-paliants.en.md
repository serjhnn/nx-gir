# Nerses Paliants

years: 14th century
role: historian, monk
source: Wikipedia

The Dominicans made him a Catholic: he belonged to the United Brothers, founded by Bartholomew of Bologna, bishop of Maragha, who sought the union of the Armenian Church with Rome. He called himself bishop of Urmia. He visited Pope Clement V in Avignon and wrote and translated there.

He wrote a history of the kings of Cilicia, above all their dealings with the Mongols. It is a valuable source but often unreliable; parts of it were later added to Smbat Sparapet's chronicle.
