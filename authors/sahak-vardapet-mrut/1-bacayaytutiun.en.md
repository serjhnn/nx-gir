# Exposition

written: 9th century
kind: a treatise on the creed
source: the book itself

"An exposition of the theology of the holy fathers, by the apostolic traditions of the Church of Christ, with a confession of the true orthodox faith of the Armenians", against the Nestorians, who held two natures in Christ. It goes from King Abgar, who believed without seeing, and Gregory the Illuminator to the teaching of the Armenian Church.

Its heading names "the holy vardapet Sahak, Catholicos of the Armenians and great Translator"; Digilib places it among the works of Sahak Mrut. Here it is in Digilib's text, one part.
