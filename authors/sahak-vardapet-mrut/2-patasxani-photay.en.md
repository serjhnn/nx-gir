# Reply to the Letter of Photius

written: 9th century
kind: a letter on the creed
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the book itself

"The reply to the letter of Photius, written by Sahak, vardapet of the Armenians, at the command of Ashot, prince of princes of the Armenians." Patriarch Photius of Constantinople had written to Ashot Bagratuni to win the Armenians over to the Council of Chalcedon; Sahak answers, defending the creed of the Armenian Church and rejecting the council's decisions.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part.
