# Reply to the Letter of King Ashot

written: 9th century
kind: a letter on soul and body
source: the book itself

"The reply to the letter of King Ashot, written by Sahak, vardapet of the Armenians." The king had asked about the passions of the soul and of the body; Sahak answers in plain words, he says, so that the king may grasp them easily: how the body's mixture of humours, its yellow and black bile, shapes its likeness and its powers, and how the soul works through it, like a musician through his instrument.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part.
