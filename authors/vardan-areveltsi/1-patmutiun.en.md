# History

written: 13th century
kind: a world history
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The Historical Compilation, one of the first Armenian attempts at a history of the world: from the creation ("In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth") and the first peoples, through the kings of Armenia and of the nations around it, to Vardan's own day under Mongol rule.

It opens with the preface of the printed edition, about 20,000 letters in Grabar, with notes on the author's life. Here it is in Digilib's text, 101 sections in two parts, with Digilib's page numbers and brackets.
