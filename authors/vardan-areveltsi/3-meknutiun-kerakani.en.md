# Commentary on Grammar

written: 13th century
kind: a commentary on grammar
source: the book itself

A commentary on the old Armenian Grammar, the translation of Dionysius Thrax. It quotes the textbook line by line in quotation marks, "Grammar is the knowledge of what is said by poets and writers", and its six parts, reading with right accent, explaining the figures, the words and stories, etymology, analogy and judging poems, and explains each.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 21 sections in one part.
