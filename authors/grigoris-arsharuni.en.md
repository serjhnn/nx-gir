# Grigoris Arsharuni

years: mid-7th – early 8th century
role: theologian, chorbishop
source: Armenian Wikipedia

He was ordained bishop under Catholicos Sahak III Dzoraporetsi and became chorbishop of the district of Arsharunik. He worked with Catholicos Hovhan Odznetsi; his contemporaries called him "a brave vardapet and a chosen philosopher". In 726 he took part in the Council of Manazkert.

At the request of Vahan Kamsarakan he wrote the Commentary on the Lectionary (Meknutiun entertsuatsots), the first Armenian commentary on the readings of the Church, which also explains the meaning of feasts and rites. It has been translated into French.
