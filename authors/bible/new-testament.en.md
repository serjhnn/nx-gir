# New Testament

written: the Armenian translation, 5th century
kind: the Gospels
source: Wikipedia · books/SOURCES.md

The four Gospels, of Matthew, Mark, Luke and John, in the Grabar of the Armenian Bible, translated in the 5th century by Mesrop Mashtots, Sahak Partev and their pupils soon after the Armenian letters were invented.

The text is the Venice edition of 1895, edited by Arsen Bagratuni, one verse to a line, each chapter headed Գլուխ Ա, Բ, …; the footnote marks were taken out. Here are only the Gospels of the New Testament.
