# Old Testament

written: the Armenian translation, 5th century
kind: a psalm
source: Wikipedia · books/SOURCES.md

Psalm 118, "Blessed are the undefiled in the way", the longest of the psalms, in the Grabar of the Armenian Bible. The Armenian and Greek Bibles number the psalms differently from the Hebrew, where it is Psalm 119.

The text is the Venice edition of 1895, edited by Arsen Bagratuni, one verse to a line. Beside it, line for line, is the modern Eastern Armenian translation of Etchmiadzin (1994); only this psalm of it is here, as that translation is not in the public domain.
