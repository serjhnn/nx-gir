# David the Invincible

years: 6th century
role: philosopher
source: Wikipedia

A Neoplatonic philosopher, he studied under Olympiodorus in Alexandria and was known as an expert on Aristotle's Physics. Armenian tradition makes him a native of Taron and explains the name "Invincible" by his skill in argument. Tradition also says that he came back to Armenia, taught there, and died in exile at Haghpat.

His works were written in Greek and survive in Armenian translation. They are handbooks for beginners: the Definitions of Philosophy and commentaries on Aristotle's Analytics and Categories and on Porphyry's Introduction (Isagoge).
