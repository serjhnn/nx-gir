# Homilies

written: 5th century
kind: homilies
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Five homilies of Mambre the Reader: two on the raising of Lazarus, one on the great day of Christ's entry into Jerusalem, a short one on the Saviour's coming to Jerusalem (sometimes taken as part of the one before), and one on the Nativity and the Baptism. His interpretation is allegorical, in the Armenian of the early Hellenizing school.

The first homilies were printed by the Mekhitarists in Venice in 1833 and 1894. Here they are in Digilib's text, one part; Digilib marks the book "mixed", with a modern foreword or notes beside the Grabar.
