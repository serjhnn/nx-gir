# Agathangelos

years: 5th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia

Agathangelos means "bearer of good news" in Greek: it is a pen name. The author calls himself a secretary of King Trdat III, but the book was written in the second half of the 5th century.

His History of the Armenians tells of Gregory the Illuminator, King Trdat and the maidens of Hripsime, and how Armenia took the new faith at the start of the 4th century. It is the main source for those events. The book was soon translated into Greek, Arabic, Syriac, Georgian and other languages.
