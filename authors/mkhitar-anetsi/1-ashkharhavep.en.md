# Ashkharhavep

written: 12th century
kind: a history of the world and of Armenia
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The Book of the Spectacles of World Events, written at the request of the vardapet Grigor of Harichavank. It probably had three parts: from King Paruyr to King Gagik I, the events of the 11th and 12th centuries, and the Crusades. Only the first chapters of the first part survive.

What is left is here: the lists of the first kings of the Chaldeans with their years in sars, the line of Japheth, the forefather of the Armenians, and the old kings down to Abgar. Here it is in Digilib's text, 22 sections in one part, with Digilib's page marks.
