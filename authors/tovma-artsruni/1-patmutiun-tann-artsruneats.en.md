# History of the House of Artsrunik

written: from the 870s to after 905
kind: a history of Vaspurakan and of Armenia
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Despite its title, this history in four books tells of all Armenia. It traces the Artsruni line from its legendary beginnings and goes on to the Arab rule; its most valuable chapters are on the expedition of Bugha in 851 and the rise of the Bagratid kingdom. It also describes King Gagik's palace and church on the island of Aghtamar.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 64 sections in four parts; its chapters start again at Ա in each book.
