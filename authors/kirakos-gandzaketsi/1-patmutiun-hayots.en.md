# History of the Armenians

written: around 1241 – 1265
kind: a history of Armenia and of the Mongol invasions
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

It sums up Armenian history from the 4th to the 12th century and then tells the events of the author's own days in detail: the Georgian and Zakarid princes, the coming of the Mongols, the captivity of Kirakos and his teacher Vanakan in 1236, and the Mongol rule. It is a primary source on the Mongol invasions and keeps the first written list of Mongolian words.

It has been translated into Latin, French, Russian and other languages. Here it is in Digilib's text, 66 sections in three parts.
