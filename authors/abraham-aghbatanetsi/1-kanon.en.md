# Canon

written: around 608
kind: a canon on marriage
source: the book itself

"On the judgements of marriage and the degrees of kinship": a letter-canon sent, it says, in the eighteenth year of Khosrow son of Hormizd, as to all the fellow bishops of the see. It sets out which kin may not marry, with proofs from the law and the prophets.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, in numbered verses.
