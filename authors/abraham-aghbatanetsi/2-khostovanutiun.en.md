# Confession

written: catholicos 607 – 615
kind: a confession of faith
source: the book itself

"The Confession of Lord Abraham, Catholicos of the Armenians": he confesses the faith as taught by the Scriptures and the fathers of the councils of Nicaea, Constantinople and Ephesus, and anathematizes Arius, Macedonius, Paul of Samosata, Mani, Marcion, Bardaisan, Eutyches and others.

It belongs to the years of his quarrel with Kyrion and the Georgian Church. Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, in numbered verses.
