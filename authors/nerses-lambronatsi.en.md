# Nerses Lambronatsi

years: 1153 – 1198
role: archbishop of Tarsus, writer, translator
source: Wikipedia

The son of Oshin II, lord of Lambron, and a nephew of Nerses Shnorhali, he studied at Skevra and at Hromkla under his uncle and Grigor Tgha, and knew Greek, Latin and Syriac. He was ordained in 1169 and became archbishop of Tarsus in 1176.

He championed the union of the churches: his address at the Council of Hromkla in 1179 is held a masterpiece of eloquence. He wrote and translated many works; in the Library are his Reflections on the Rites of the Church, a commentary on the services. The Armenian Church counts him among its saints.
