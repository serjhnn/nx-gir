# Kirakos Gandzaketsi

years: c. 1200 – 1271
role: historian, vardapet
source: Wikipedia

Born near Gandzak, he studied at Nor Getik and then at Khoranashat under the vardapet Vanakan. In 1236 he and his teacher were taken captive by the Mongols.

His History of the Armenians sums up the 4th to the 12th centuries and tells the events of his own days in detail. It is a primary source on the Mongol invasions and keeps the first written list of Mongolian words. It has been translated into Latin, French, Russian and other languages.
