# History

written: 1072 – 1079
kind: a history of the 11th century
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The History of the sufferings brought by the foreign peoples around us, written as an eyewitness, without a patron. In twenty-five chapters it tells of Bagratid Armenia's dealings with Byzantium and Georgia, the Seljuk invasions of 1047–1048, the fall of Ani in 1064 and the battle of Manzikert in 1071. It opens: "The days of suffering have come upon us."

Here it is in Digilib's text, two parts.
