# Hetum the Historian

years: 13th–14th century
role: historian, lord of Korikos
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

Of the Hetumid house of Cilicia, he was the son of Oshin, prince of Korikos, and a nephew of King Hetum I and of Smbat Sparapet. He was lord of Korikos and fought the Mamluks of Egypt, then left the world and became a monk in Cyprus.

In 1307 at Poitiers, at the request of Pope Clement V, he dictated in French the Flower of the Histories of the East, a history of Asia and the Mongols that did much to shape how Europe saw the East. Under his name a chronicle and genealogies of the Rubenid and Hetumid houses have come down in Armenian.
