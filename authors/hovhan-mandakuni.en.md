# Hovhan Mandakuni

years: catholicos 478 – 490
role: Catholicos of All Armenians, hymn writer
source: Armenian Wikipedia

Born in the village of Tsakhnot in Arshamunik, of the noble house of the Mandakunis, he was a pupil of Sahak Partev and Mesrop Mashtots. He supported Vahan Mamikonian's revolt against the Persians, cheering the soldiers with his presence and his prayers.

As catholicos he rebuilt the cathedrals of Vagharshapat and Dvin and set the order of the liturgy and the hours. He wrote homilies, prayers and seventeen hymns, among them those to the Holy Translators and to Mashtots. His canons are in the Library.
