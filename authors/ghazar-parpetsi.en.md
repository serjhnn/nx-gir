# Ghazar Parpetsi

years: mid-5th – early 6th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia

Born in the village of Parpi near Ashtarak, he grew up in the house of the Mamikonians. Vahan Mamikonian called him to Vagharshapat to oversee the rebuilding of a monastery, but the clergy accused him of heresy and he left for Amida. There he wrote his Letter to Vahan Mamikonian, a defence of himself.

Around 493 Vahan called him back and asked him to write a history of Armenia. His History of the Armenians runs from 387 to 485, from the partition of Armenia to Vahan's appointment as marzpan. With Yeghishe's, it is the main source on the war of Vardan.
