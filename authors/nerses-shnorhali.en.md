# Nerses Shnorhali

years: 1102 – 1173
role: Catholicos of All Armenians, poet, theologian
source: Wikipedia

Of the Pahlavuni family, the son of Apirat Pahlavuni and great-grandson of Grigor Magistros, he was Catholicos of All Armenians from 1166 to 1173, at Hromkla. He negotiated with Emperor Manuel Komnenos for the union of the Armenian and Greek churches, but the emperor's terms were unacceptable and the talks failed. The Armenian and Catholic churches count him among the saints.

He is remembered as a theologian, poet and writer of hymns. In the Library are his Lament on Edessa, on the fall of the city, his Vipasanutiun, his General Epistle on the life of the Church and of society, his odes and songs, On the Heavens, and his riddles.
