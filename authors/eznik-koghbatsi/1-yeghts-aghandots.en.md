# Refutation of the Sects

written: 441–449
kind: a polemical work
source: Wikipedia
status: machine

Eznik Koghbatsi's work is in four books: the first against the pagans, the second against the Persian religion (Zurvanism), the third against the Greek philosophers, the fourth against the sect of Marcion.

It is one of the finest examples of the Grabar of the Golden Age. It was first printed in Smyrna in 1762; every edition comes from one manuscript of 1280.
