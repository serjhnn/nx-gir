# Severian of Gabala

years: 355 – 408/425
role: bishop of Gabala, preacher
source: Wikipedia · the preface of the Digilib edition

Bishop of Gabala in Syria and, the Armenian editor says, a native of Emesa. Around 400–404 he was a popular preacher in Constantinople and a favourite of Empress Eudoxia. He later became an enemy of John Chrysostom and helped to condemn him.

More than fifty of his sermons survive; some are lost in Greek and kept only in translation, Armenian among them. Eight of his sermons were published in Venice in 1827 by Mkrtich Avgerian (J. B. Aucher) from the old Armenian translation.
