# Chronicle

written: late 13th – early 14th century
kind: a chronicle
source: the book itself

A chronicle that has come down under Hetum's name, year by year from the birth of Christ ("In the first year of the birth of Christ"), each year with its number in letters and in figures, and more fully as it comes nearer to Cilicia and the author's own time.

Here it is in Digilib's text, four sections ([1] to [4]) in one part, with no headings of its own.
