# Genealogies

written: late 13th – early 14th century
kind: genealogies of the Cilician and Crusader houses
source: the book itself

Four short genealogies that have come down under Hetum's name: the history of the house of the Rubenids, how they came to rule Cilicia; the genealogy of the kings of Jerusalem and of Cyprus; the kings of Cyprus; and the genealogy of the princes of Antioch, each line a generation.

Here they are in Digilib's text, one part.
