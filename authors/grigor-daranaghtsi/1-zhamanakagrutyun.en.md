# Chronicle

written: 1634 – 1640
kind: a chronicle of the 16th and 17th centuries
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Written as an eyewitness of its events: the Jelali revolts, the inner life of the Ottoman Empire, the condition of the Armenians in Constantinople, the Crimea, Jerusalem and Armenia, and the author's own journeys and work, among them his years leading the Armenians of Rodosto.

Its author is seen as the first figure of the revival of Armenian historical writing. Here it is in Digilib's text, six parts, in [1], [2] sections with no headings, followed by a list of the manuscripts of the Jerusalem library.
