# Chronicle

written: begun around 1113; carried on by Grigor the Priest
kind: a chronicle of the 10th to 12th centuries
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The Chronicle of Matthew of Edessa covers about two centuries from the 950s: the Bagratid kingdom and its fall, the wars of Byzantines and Arabs, the coming of the Seljuks and the first Crusades, with the Crusader county of Edessa. Each entry names its year in the Armenian era, in letters after a hyphen with the thousand left out (-ՆԱ, 401 = 952).

Here it is in Digilib's text, four parts: three of the chronicle, and the fourth the continuation by Grigor the Priest. The Contents list has an entry for each line that names a year.
