# Tovma Artsruni

years: 9th–10th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia

Of the Artsruni house, he was a relative of King Gagik I of Vaspurakan. He began the History of the House of Artsrunik in the 870s and finished it after 905.

Despite its title, this history in four books tells of all Armenia. Its most valuable chapters are on Arab rule, the expedition of Bugha in 851 and the rise of the Bagratid kingdom. It also describes Gagik's palace and church on the island of Aghtamar.
