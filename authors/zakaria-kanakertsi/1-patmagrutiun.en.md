# History

written: second half of the 17th century
kind: a history of Persia, Armenia and Hovhannavank
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The history of Zakaria's own time: the order of the kings of Persia and of Armenia, the reigns of Jahan Shah, Yaqub, Ismail, Tahmasp and the shahs after them, and the cruelty of Persian rule, which the author saw himself, together with the life of the monastery of Hovhannavank, where he spent his whole life.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 135 sections in three parts; three are empty.
