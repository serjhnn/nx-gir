# Works

written: first half of the 8th century (most in 726 – 727)
kind: doctrinal letters and treatises
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The doctrinal works of Khosrovik, mostly letters, written around the Council of Manazkert of 726. Against the creed of Chalcedon and against the two Syrian sects at the other extreme, the Severians and the Julianists, they defend the creed and rites of the Armenian Church.

This is a critical edition: after a foreword and five chapters, each section ends with the variant readings of the manuscripts, marked Ա, Բ, Գ, Դ. Here it is in Digilib's text, two parts.
