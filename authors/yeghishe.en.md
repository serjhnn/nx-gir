# Yeghishe

years: 5th century
role: historian, vardapet
source: Wikipedia

By tradition he was one of the younger pupils of Sahak Partev and Mesrop Mashtots. He studied in Alexandria, then served as secretary to Vardan Mamikonian and took part in the war of 449–451. After Avarayr he left military life and lived as a hermit in the mountains of Rshtunik.

At the request of David Mamikonian he wrote the History of Vardan and the Armenian War, about the battle of Avarayr and the Armenian revolt. The Commentary on Genesis, the Admonitions and the Canons are also ascribed to him.
