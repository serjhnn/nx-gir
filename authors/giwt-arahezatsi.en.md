# Giwt Arahezatsi

years: c. 400 – 478
role: Catholicos of All Armenians
source: Armenian Wikipedia

Born in the village of Arahez in Tayk, he was one of the younger pupils of Sahak Partev and Mesrop Mashtots. He was bishop of Vanand, and in 461 he was elected catholicos. He set the day of the martyrs of Avarayr in the church calendar.

In 471 King Peroz summoned him to Ctesiphon and deposed him, but the discontent in Armenia was so great that he was allowed to return. He spent the rest of his life in the village of Otmus in Vanand, where he is buried. The Armenian Church counts him among its saints.
