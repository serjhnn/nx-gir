# Arakel Davrizhetsi

years: 1590s – 1670
role: historian, vardapet
source: Wikipedia

Born in Tabriz (Davrezh in the Armenian of his time), he studied at Etchmiadzin and became a vardapet. In 1636 he was abbot of Hovhannavank for a year, then went as envoy of Catholicos Pilippos to Isfahan, Amasya, Sebastia, Urfa, Aleppo, Jerusalem and elsewhere. He died at Etchmiadzin.

In 1662 he finished his Book of Histories, the history of Armenia and its neighbours from 1602 to 1662, including the forced deportation of the Armenians under Shah Abbas I. He was the first Armenian historian whose book was printed: in Amsterdam in 1669, at the press of Voskan Yerevantsi.
