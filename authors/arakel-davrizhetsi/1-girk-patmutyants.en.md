# Book of Histories

written: finished in 1662
kind: a history of Armenia and its neighbours, 1602 – 1662
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The history of Armenia and the lands around it from 1602 to 1662: the troubles of the catholicoi, the coming of Shah Abbas I and the forced deportation of the Armenians to Persia, the Jelali raids, the taking of Ganja and Shamakhi, and much of the life of the Church in his time.

It was the first book by an Armenian historian to be printed, in Amsterdam in 1669, at the press of Voskan Yerevantsi. Here it is in Digilib's text, seven parts; Digilib lists its sections out of order, so they were fetched in the order of their numbers.
