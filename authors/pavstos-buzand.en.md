# Pavstos Buzand

years: 5th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia

The Epic Histories (Buzandaran Patmutiwnk), probably written in the 470s, are ascribed to him. Whether an author named Pavstos really existed is disputed today: the book may be the work of an anonymous cleric.

It tells of 4th-century Armenia, from the death of Gregory the Illuminator to 387, when Armenia was divided between Persia and Rome. It is written in the manner of oral epic and weaves three stories together: the Arsacid kings, the patriarchs of the house of Gregory, and the Mamikonian commanders.
