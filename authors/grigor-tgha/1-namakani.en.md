# Letters

written: catholicos 1173 – 1193
kind: a letter on the creed
source: the book itself

A long reply to "the vardapets of the Armenians of the northern parts", who had written against him. They had cursed the Tome of Leo and called his side Nestorians; Grigor asks what the Tome is, defends his drawing near to the Greek Church, and argues with them from Athanasius and the hymns and prayers of the Armenian Church on the one Christ and His Passion.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one part.
