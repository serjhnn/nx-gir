# Chronicle (Digilib)

written: 13th century
kind: a chronicle
source: Wikipedia
status: machine

Smbat Sparapet's Chronicle tells the events from 951/952 to 1274, of Armenia, Cilicia and the lands around them, year by year. An anonymous writer carried it on to the 1330s.

This is the same Chronicle in Digilib's text, cut into numbered sections.
