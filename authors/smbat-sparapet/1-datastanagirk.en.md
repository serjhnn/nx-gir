# Code of Law

written: 13th century
kind: a code of law
source: Wikipedia
status: machine

Smbat Sparapet reworked the Code of Law of Mkhitar Gosh for the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia, fitting it to the life of his time and country.

It is written in Middle Armenian, close to the spoken language of Cilicia, not in Grabar. Smbat also translated the Assizes of Antioch from French.
