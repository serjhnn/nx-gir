# Grigor Tatevatsi

years: 1346 – 1409
role: vardapet, philosopher, theologian
source: Wikipedia

Born in Syunik. He studied at the monasteries of Tatev and Metsop, then taught at the university of Tatev, which reached its greatest fame in his time. He stood for the independence of the Armenian Church and wrote against union with Rome.

In 1397 he finished the Book of Questions (Girk Harcmanc), a great work of theology built of questions and answers. In 1407 he finished his Book of Sermons, more than three hundred sermons in a Winter and a Summer volume. He also wrote commentaries.
