# Hakob Ghrimetsi

years: 14th–15th century
role: calendar scholar, philosopher, teacher
source: Armenian Wikipedia

Born in the Crimea, he studied and taught at the school of Yerznka, where he turned his lectures into a clear handbook of the calendar. In 1410 he moved to the new school of the monastery of Metsop as its head teacher, and taught philosophy and the calendar.

In 1416 he set right the corrupted textbook of the calendar and wrote his Commentary on the Calendar, drawing also on his own observations of the phases of the moon. He was also a musician, grammarian and commentator, and taught the art of the scribe.
