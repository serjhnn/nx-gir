# Grigor Narekatsi

years: c. 950 – 1003/1011
role: poet, monk, theologian
source: Wikipedia

He lived in Vaspurakan, on the southern shore of Lake Van. His mother died when he was small, and he grew up and studied at the monastery of Narek, where he spent his whole life.

His Book of Lamentations, which people call simply the Narek, is one of the summits of medieval Armenian literature and for centuries was the book kept in Armenian homes. He also wrote odes, songs and panegyrics. In 2015 he was declared a Doctor of the Church.
