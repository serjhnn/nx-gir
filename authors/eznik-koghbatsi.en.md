# Eznik of Kolb

years: c. 380 – 450
role: translator, theologian, bishop
source: Wikipedia

Born in the village of Kolb in Ayrarat. A pupil of Sahak Partev and Mesrop Mashtots, he studied at the school of Ashtishat. His teachers sent him to Edessa and Constantinople to bring back Greek and Syriac manuscripts, and he took part in translating the Bible. He is probably the Eznik who, as bishop of Bagrevand, attended the Synod of Artashat in 449.

His one surviving work, the Refutation of the Sects (Eghts aghandots), argues against the pagans, the religion of the Persians, the Greek philosophers and Marcion. It is one of the finest examples of classical Armenian prose.
