# Admonitions

written: 5th century (ascribed)
kind: homilies and a commentary
source: the book itself

Writings ascribed to Yeghishe, gathered under one name. The first part opens with an address on the life of hermits and goes on to the Commentary on Joshua and Judges, which explains the crossing of the Jordan, the fall of Jericho and the other stories of the two books, chapter by chapter, down to Samson.

The second and third parts hold a teaching on the Passion according to John, in lettered chapters, and two short homilies, on the remembrance of the dead and on the Judgement and the Second Coming. Here they are in Digilib's text, three parts.
