# Canons

written: 5th century (ascribed)
kind: church canons
source: the book itself

The canons "spoken by Yeghishe vardapet": short rules on bodily purity, on when one who has become unclean, by day or by night, may enter the church and take communion, and on the penance for it.

The rules are numbered one after another, as Digilib has them. Here they are in Digilib's text, one short part.
