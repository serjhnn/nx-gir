# Commentary on Genesis

written: 5th century (ascribed)
kind: a commentary on the Book of Genesis
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

A commentary on Genesis ascribed to Yeghishe, which explains the creation and the stories of the first book of the Bible verse by verse, often with Greek words and a moral sense. It survives only in fragments.

This is a critical edition of those fragments, in 108 sections and three parts, with the editor's notes and an appendix; Digilib lists the first sections out of order, so they were fetched in the order of their numbers. It is the one of Yeghishe's books with a translation into modern Armenian here.
