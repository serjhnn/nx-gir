# On Vardan

written: 5th century
kind: a history of the war of 449 – 451
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The History of Vardan and the Armenian War, written at the request of David Mamikonian. It tells why the Persian king Yazdegerd II tried to force Zoroastrianism on the Armenians, how the princes and the clergy rose against him, the betrayal of Vasak Syuni, the battle of Avarayr in 451 where Vardan Mamikonian fell, and the captivity and martyrdom of the clergy that followed. Its seven chapters are called "ways" (yeghanak).

The book shaped how Armenians remember Vardan and Avarayr, and the Church keeps the day of the battle as a feast. Here it is in Digilib's text, eight sections in two parts.
