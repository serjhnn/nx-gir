# Ukhtanes

years: c. 935 – 1000
role: historian, bishop of Sebastia
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

He studied at the monastery of Narek under its founder Anania Narekatsi, then became bishop of Sebastia and probably of Edessa. He wrote his history at Anania's request.

His History was in three parts: the patriarchs and kings of Armenia, the separation of the Georgians and the Armenians, and the baptism of the people called Tsad. The last part is lost.
