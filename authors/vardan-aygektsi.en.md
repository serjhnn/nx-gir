# Vardan Aygektsi

years: died 1250
role: vardapet, fabulist
source: Wikipedia

Born in the village of Maratha in Cilicia, he studied at the monastery of Arkakaghin and became a vardapet. He worked in Amid, attended the coronation of King Levon in 1198, and from 1208 lived at the monastery of Aygek in the Amanus mountains.

He wrote The Root of Faith, the principles of the creed of the Armenian Church. He is known for his fables: a large collection of them was printed in Amsterdam in 1668 as the Fox Book, because the fox is the hero of so many. In 1975 Robert Sahakyants made the cartoon The Fox Book from them.
