# Odes and Songs

written: 10th century
kind: spiritual poems
source: Wikipedia
place: narek
status: machine

The poems of Grigor Narekatsi: tagher (odes) and gandzer (songs). His two dozen or so odes are the first known religious poems in Armenian literature; he also wrote music for them.

The gandzer are spiritual songs, in verse and in prose. In the odes the pictures of nature, the rose, the sun, the sea, serve the joy of a feast. Here they are numbered in letters, with titles in capitals.
