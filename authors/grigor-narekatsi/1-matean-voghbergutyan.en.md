# Book of Lamentations

written: c. 1002–1003
kind: a poem of prayers
source: Wikipedia
place: narek
status: machine

Grigor Narekatsi finished it at the end of his life, at the monastery of Narek. The book has 95 chapters and more than ten thousand lines; each chapter is a prayer "from the depths of the heart". People call it simply the Narek.

For centuries it was kept in Armenian homes as a prayer book. Voskan Yerevantsi tried to print it in Marseille in 1673. It is one of the summits of medieval Armenian literature.
