# Seal of Faith

written: early 7th century (catholicos 613 – 628)
kind: a florilegium of the fathers
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

"The Seal of Faith of the universal holy Church, of the confessions of our orthodox fathers, gathered in the days of Catholicos Komitas", to keep the faith of the Armenian Church. It opens with the compiler's list of chapters: ten "words" (ban), from the confession of the Holy Trinity and the oneness of Christ to the incorruptible body, His will and His Passion.

Under each word come quotations from the fathers, Dionysius, Ephrem the Syrian, Hippolytus, Gregory the Wonderworker and many more, numbered afresh with each author. Here it is in Digilib's text, four parts.
