# Grigor Daranaghtsi

years: born 1576
role: chronicler, vardapet
source: Armenian Wikipedia

Born in the town of Kamakh in Daranagh, in Upper Armenia, he is also called Kamakhetsi. He studied in Baberd and lived at the monastery of the Illuminator on Mount Sepuh, in eastern Armenia, in the Crimea, in Jerusalem and in Constantinople, and for many years led the Armenians of Rodosto.

In 1634–1640 he wrote his Chronicle, as an eyewitness of its events. It is rich in news of the Jelali revolts, of the inner life of the Ottoman Empire and of the condition of the Armenians. He is seen as the first figure of the revival of Armenian historical writing.
