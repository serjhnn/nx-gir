# Relief of Fevers

written: 12th century
kind: a book of medicine
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

An encyclopedic book on fevers by the father of Armenian medicine: what a fever is and how it is defined, then the kinds of fevers, one-day fevers from heat, from cold, from fatigue and other causes, and the longer ones, with their signs and their cure. It also speaks of diet, of surgery and of the healing of the mind.

A complete manuscript was found in Constantinople in 1727 and is now in Paris. Here it is in Digilib's text, 49 sections in two parts; the first section, a title, is empty, and the last, ՆՈՒՍԽԱՅՔ, is a list of remedies.
