# Abraham Aghbatanetsi

years: catholicos 607 – 615
role: Catholicos of All Armenians
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

Born in the mid-6th century in the village of Aghbatan in Rshtunik, Vaspurakan, he was bishop of Rshtunik. In 607, after Vrtanes Kertogh's time as locum tenens, he was elected catholicos at the initiative of Smbat Bagratuni.

In his time the Georgian Church under Catholicos Kyrion accepted the Council of Chalcedon and broke with the Armenian Church; Abraham tried in vain, with letters and councils, to restore the union. His canon on marriage between kin and his Confession are in the Library.
