# Odes

written: 12th century
kind: songs
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Shnorhali's tagher, songs for the feasts: the Annunciation, the lighting of the lamps of the Nativity, the saints and more, each followed by its pokh, a short refrain.

Here they are in Digilib's text, 102 sections in one part, one verse to a line.
