# General Epistle

written: 12th century (catholicos 1166 – 1173)
kind: a pastoral letter
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

"Nerses, servant of Christ and by His mercy Catholicos of the Armenians", writes to the whole nation, order by order: to the monks and the hermits, to the bishops, the priests and the vardapets, to the princes, the soldiers, the townsfolk and merchants, the farmers and the women. He tells each how to live and what to mend.

It is a picture of the life of the Church and of society in his time. Here it is in Digilib's text, one part, in long paragraphs.
