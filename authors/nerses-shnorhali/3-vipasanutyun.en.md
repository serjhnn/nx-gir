# Vipasanutiun

written: 1121
kind: a history in verse
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

A history in verse, "a son is a debtor to his father": the story of the Armenians told as a long poem, from the first kings and Abgar through Gregory the Illuminator and the patriarchs after him. It ends with its date, the Armenian year 570 (1121), and calls its author a humble novice.

Here it is in Digilib's text, two sections in one part, one verse to a line.
