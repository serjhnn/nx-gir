# Gandzer

written: 12th century
kind: hymns
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Gandzer, tagher and pokher: hymns for the feasts of the Church, for the Nativity, the Theophany and the saints, many still sung in the Armenian Church. Shnorhali is remembered above all as a writer of hymns.

Here they are in Digilib's text, fourteen sections in one part. Their numbers start at [ՃԳ], as on Digilib, and one section is only a group heading of the appendices, with no text of its own.
