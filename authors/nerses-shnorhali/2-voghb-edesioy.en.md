# Lament on Edessa

written: after 1144
kind: a lament in verse
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

A long poem on the fall of Edessa to Zengi in 1144. The city itself speaks: it calls on the churches, its sister cities and all peoples to weep with it, tells of its glory and its taking, and ends with praise of the Father, the Son and the Spirit.

Here it is in Digilib's text, fourteen sections in one part, one verse to a line.
