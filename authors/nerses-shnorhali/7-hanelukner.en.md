# Riddles

written: 12th century
kind: riddles in verse
source: the Digilib edition

Riddles in verse, "spoken by Lord Nerses", each a few lines that describe a thing without naming it.

They are not in Grabar but in Middle Armenian, the language of the 12th and 13th centuries, and are kept here with his other works. Here they are in Digilib's text, one short part.
