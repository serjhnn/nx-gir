# History of the Tatars

written: 13th century
kind: a history of the Mongols
source: the book itself

Also known as the History of the Nation of the Archers, from its first chapter, "On the nation of the archers". It tells who the Mongols were, their way of life, their faith and their laws, their first war with the Georgians, the comet that foretold their return, the subjection of the Georgian and Armenian princes, the wars and ravages of Baiju, and what followed, in seventeen chapters.
