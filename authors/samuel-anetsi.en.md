# Samuel Anetsi

years: 12th century
role: chronicler, priest
source: Armenian Wikipedia

Born in Ani or near it, he was a pupil of Hovhannes Sarkavag and a priest.

At the request of Catholicos Grigor Tgha he wrote his Collection from the Books of Historians, a short chronicle from Adam to 1180; others carried it on to 1665. He is rare among medieval historians in giving tables that compare different systems of dating, and in naming his sources.
