# Winter Volume

written: finished in 1407
kind: a book of sermons
source: Wikipedia
place: tatew
status: machine

The Book of Sermons of Grigor Tatevatsi is 344 sermons in two volumes, winter and summer. This volume holds the sermons for the feasts and Sundays of the winter half of the year.

The sermons were read in monasteries and churches and taught the Bible and the teaching of the Church. The summer half is in the Summer Volume.
