# Summer Volume

written: finished in 1407
kind: a book of sermons
source: Wikipedia
place: tatew
status: machine

The second volume of the Book of Sermons of Grigor Tatevatsi: its 344 sermons are split into a winter and a summer volume. Here are the sermons for the feasts and Sundays of the summer half of the year.

It is the most translated book of our library: its parts can be read in translation beside the original.
