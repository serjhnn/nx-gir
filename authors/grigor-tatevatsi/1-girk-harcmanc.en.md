# Book of Questions

written: 1397
kind: a summa of theology
source: Wikipedia
place: tatew
status: machine

Grigor Tatevatsi finished it in 1397, at the school of Tatev. It is written in questions and answers (Prak, Question, Answer), the way a lesson went at the school.

It defends the teaching of the Armenian Church against Islam and the Unitors, who spread the Latin creed in Armenia. It sums up medieval Armenian thought: theology, philosophy, natural science.
