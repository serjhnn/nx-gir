# Frequent Discourses

written: ascribed to Gregory (4th century)
kind: homilies
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The Yachakhapatum chark, a book of homilies ascribed to Gregory the Illuminator: on the Holy Trinity and the faith, rebukes of errant minds, the truth and its guides, the hidden things that are revealed, and the life a Christian should lead. Each opens with an "Ա. Title" line and then its number.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 25 sections in three parts. Three headings have broken letters on Digilib and are left as they are.
