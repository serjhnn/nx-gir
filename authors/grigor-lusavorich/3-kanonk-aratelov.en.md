# Canons of Gregory the Apostle

written: ascribed to Gregory (4th century)
kind: church canons
source: the book itself

"The canons of Saint Gregory, the Apostle of the Armenians", thirty chapters by their heading: rules for priests who sin, who take a wife, who lend at interest, for those who eat in the houses of pagans, and the like, each a line.

Here they are in Digilib's text, one short part.
