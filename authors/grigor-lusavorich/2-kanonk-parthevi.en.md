# Canons of Gregory the Parthian

written: ascribed to Gregory (4th century)
kind: canons in questions and answers
source: the book itself

"The canons of Saint Gregory the Parthian, and again his questions and answers": short teachings set as questions and answers, numbered one after another. It begins: "Who is the father of death? Envy."

Here they are in Digilib's text, one short part.
