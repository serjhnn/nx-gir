# Sahak Vardapet

years: 9th century
role: theologian, bishop
source: Armenian Wikipedia

He was bishop of Ashunk in Tayk. Persecuted by the Georgian Church, he moved to Shirakavan; it was then that the Georgians called him Mrut, "unbeliever".

He is known for his reply to Patriarch Photius, written at the command of Ashot Bagratuni in answer to the patriarch's letter to Ashot. In it he defends the creed of the Armenian Church and rejects the decisions of the Council of Chalcedon. His Exposition (Batsayaytutiun) and his reply to Ashot's letter are in the Library too.
