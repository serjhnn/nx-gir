# Sebeos

years: 7th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia

The real author's name is not known; he was probably a cleric. The history survives in a manuscript written at Baghesh (Bitlis) in 1672, with no name or title. The name Sebeos (Eusebius) was given to it in the 19th century, after "Bishop Sebeos of the Bagratunis", who signed at the Council of Dvin in 645, but this is only a guess.

The book is the main source for Armenian history in the 6th and 7th centuries. It is the oldest surviving detailed account of the rise of Islam and the first Arab conquests, and one of the very few non-Islamic sources on them.
