# Chronicle

written: 15th century (after 1443)
kind: a short chronicle
source: the book itself

A short chronicle that lists, year by year, the reigns of the Mongol khans from Hulagu (1249) and the raids of Timur and of the Turkmens around Van, Archesh and Baghesh, down to the 1440s. Each year is given in Armenian letters and, in brackets, in our era.
