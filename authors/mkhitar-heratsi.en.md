# Mkhitar Heratsi

years: 12th century
role: physician
source: Wikipedia

Born in Her (Khoy, now in northwestern Iran), which gives him his name, he knew Persian, Greek and Arabic. He is often called the father of Armenian medicine.

His Relief of Fevers (Jermants mkhitarutiun) is an encyclopedic book on fevers that also speaks of surgery, diet and the healing of the mind. A complete manuscript was found in Constantinople in 1727 and is now in Paris. Since 1989 the medical university of Yerevan has borne his name.
