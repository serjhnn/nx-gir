# Commentary on the Lectionary

written: around 700
kind: a commentary on the readings of the Church
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The first Armenian commentary on the lectionary, written at the request of Vahan Kamsarakan. It explains the readings of the feasts and what the feasts and rites mean, from the creation of man and his fall to the feast of the Epiphany, each chapter opening with "how" or "that" (Թէ …).

It has been translated into French. Here it is in Digilib's text, 36 sections in two parts; one section, the title, is empty on Digilib.
