# Koriun

years: 5th century
role: historian, translator
source: Wikipedia

The youngest pupil of Mesrop Mashtots, he took part in his teacher's work of translation. He was born around 380–390; his name means "lion's cub".

His one known work, the Life of Mashtots (Vark Mashtotsi), is the oldest original work written in Armenian. It tells how the Armenian letters were invented, of Mashtots' preaching, and of the translation of the Bible and other books.
