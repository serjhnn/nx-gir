# Vardan Areveltsi

years: c. 1198 – 1271
role: historian, geographer, vardapet
source: Wikipedia

Born in Gandzak, he studied at Nor Getik under Mkhitar Gosh and then at Khornashat, where he also learned Hebrew, Greek, Latin and Persian. He travelled to Jerusalem and to Cilicia, to the court of King Hetum I. He founded schools in several monasteries and from 1255 taught at Khor Virap, where he died. Gevorg Skevratsi and Hovhannes Yerznkatsi were among his pupils.

In 1264 he negotiated with Hulagu Khan in Tabriz and won privileges for the Armenians under Mongol rule. He left more than a hundred and twenty works; in the Library are his Historical Compilation, one of the first Armenian attempts at a history of the world, his Geography and his commentary on grammar.
