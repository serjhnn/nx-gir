# History of the Armenians

written: second half of the 5th century
kind: a history
source: Wikipedia
place: vagharshapat, artashat
status: machine

It tells how Armenia became Christian: from the Sasanians taking the place of the Parthians (224) and the murder of Khosrov to the story of Trdat and Gregory the Illuminator, the martyrdom of Hripsime and her companions, and Aristakes at the Council of Nicaea.

It was soon translated into Greek, and from the Greek into Arabic, Latin and Ethiopic. The oldest complete manuscripts are of the 12th and 13th centuries.
