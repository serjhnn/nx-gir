# Collection

written: around 1180, carried on by others to 1665
kind: a chronicle from Adam
source: Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The Collection from the Books of Historians, a short chronicle from Adam to 1180, written at the request of Catholicos Grigor Tgha. It counts the years of the patriarchs, the kings and the catholicoi, and gives tables that compare different systems of dating, naming its sources as few medieval historians did; others carried it on to 1665.

Here it is in Digilib's text, two parts.
