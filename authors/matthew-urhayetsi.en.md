# Matthew of Edessa

years: late 11th century – 1144
role: historian, abbot
source: Wikipedia

Born in Edessa (Urha) in the second half of the 11th century, he was abbot of the Red Monastery near Kaysun. He opposed both the Greek and the Latin churches and wrote bitterly of the greed and ingratitude of the Frankish lords. He was probably killed in 1144, when Zengi besieged Edessa.

His Chronicle, begun around 1113, covers about two centuries from the mid-10th: the Bagratid kingdom, the first Crusades and the wars of Byzantines and Arabs. Grigor the Priest carried it on.
