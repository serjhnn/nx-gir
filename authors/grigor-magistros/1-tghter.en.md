# Letters

written: first half of the 11th century
kind: letters
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The letters of Grigor Magistros, prince and scholar: to Catholicos Petros, in times of unrest, about King Gagik and the affairs of the Church, and to bishops, the brethren of monasteries, a Greek scribe and others. Written in a learned, ornate style full of Greek learning, they discuss politics and philosophy and are an important source on Armenia in the years when Byzantium annexed the Bagratid kingdom.

Here they are in Digilib's text, 88 sections in three parts, with Digilib's page numbers and brackets.
