# Hovhannes Draskhanakerttsi

years: died 925
role: Catholicos of All Armenians, historian
source: Wikipedia

Catholicos from 897 to 925, he studied under his kinsman and predecessor, Catholicos Mashtots I. Under Kings Smbat I and Ashot II the Iron he helped make the Bagratid kingdom strong, made peace between Ashot and his rebel princes, and went on embassies to the neighbouring lands.

He brought the see back to Dvin, then in 924, fleeing the Sajid army, moved to Vaspurakan, where he spent his last years. He wrote a History of the Armenians and a list of the Armenian patriarchs.
