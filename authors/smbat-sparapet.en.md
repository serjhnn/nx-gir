# Smbat Sparapet

years: 1208 – 1276
role: commander, diplomat, judge, historian
source: Wikipedia

The elder brother of King Hetum I of Cilicia and the sparapet, the supreme commander, of the kingdom. He fought in many battles. In 1247–1250 he went as an envoy to the Mongols, to Karakorum, and made an agreement that protected Cilicia for years.

In 1265 he wrote his own Datastanagirk (Book of Law) in Middle Armenian, built on Mkhitar Gosh's. He also translated the Assizes of Antioch. His Chronicle is an important source for the history of Cilician Armenia.
