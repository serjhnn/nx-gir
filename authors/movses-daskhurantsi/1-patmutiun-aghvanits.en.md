# History of the Country of Aghvank

written: 10th century, from older writings
kind: a history of Caucasian Albania
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The history of Aghvank (Caucasian Albania) and the eastern provinces of Armenia, in three books. The first two reach the 7th century, the Khazar invasions among them, and keep the History of 684 whole; the third reaches the 10th century. It may be the work of several authors: the old manuscripts give no name, Mkhitar Gosh first called the author Daskhurantsi, and Kirakos Gandzaketsi Kaghankatvatsi.

Here it is in Digilib's text, three parts; Digilib lists its sections out of order, so they were fetched in the order of their numbers.
