# Homilies

written: around 400 (in Greek)
kind: homilies
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Fifteen homilies of Severian, bishop of Gabala, in their old Armenian translation: on the dispensation of the Saviour, on baptism, on the apostles, on Easter, on the martyr Akakios, on the Gospel words "Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me", on the entry into Jerusalem, on the withered fig tree and on the Passion. The last is headed as Eusebius's.

Before them stands the Armenian editor's preface: before this edition, it says, only three of these homilies were known in Europe, two mixed into the works of John Chrysostom and one taken for Basil's. Some of Severian's homilies are lost in Greek and kept only in translations such as these. Here they are in Digilib's text, three parts.
