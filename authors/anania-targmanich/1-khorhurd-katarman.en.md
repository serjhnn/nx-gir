# On the Mystery of the Death of the Apostle Peter

written: unknown
kind: a homily
source: the book itself

A homily on Peter's end: when the apostle came to the place of his death, he asked to be crucified head down. The homily explains his words before the cross and the mystery of the cross itself, a death that kills death and gives life.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, in numbered verses.
