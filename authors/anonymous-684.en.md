# Anonymous

years: 7th century
role: historian
source: the preface of the Digilib edition

The author's name is not known. The work is called by convention the History of 684, and it survives copied word for word into the History of the Aghvank of Movses Daskhurantsi. It tells of Caucasian Albania (Aghvank) from the 630s to the 680s and ends with the events of 682–683.

The author was close to the men he wrote about: the princes Javanshir and Varaz Trdat and Bishop Israel, whom he may have followed to the land of the Huns. The book also keeps Davtak Kertogh's Lament on the death of Javanshir, a rare piece of early medieval Armenian poetry.
