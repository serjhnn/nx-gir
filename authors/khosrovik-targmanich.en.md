# Khosrovik Targmanich

years: first half of the 8th century
role: writer, theologian
source: Armenian Wikipedia

The sources also call him Khosrovik the Rhetor; the years of his birth and death are unknown. With Catholicos Hovhan Odznetsi he took part in the Council of Manazkert in 726, which was to settle the quarrels between the Armenian and Syrian churches.

Seven of his doctrinal works survive, mostly letters, five of them written in 726–727. He rejects the Chalcedonian creed and the two extreme Syrian sects, the Severians and the Julianists, and defends the creed and rites of the Armenian Church.
