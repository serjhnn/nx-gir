The Code of Law of Mkhitar Gosh, the first Armenian collection of secular and Church law, was compiled at the end of the 12th century. In the 13th century it gained the force of law in Cilicia; in the 16th it was used for the Armenians of Lviv, and in the 18th it became part of Georgian law.

Edition C follows the order of edition A, but is shorter and differs in content.
