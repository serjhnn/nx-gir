# History of the Armenians

written: second half of the 10th century
kind: a history of Armenia and of the church schism
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Written at the request of Anania Narekatsi, in three parts. The first, the History of the patriarchs and kings of Armenia, starts from Adam, Noah and his sons and the division of the world. The second, the History of the separation of the Georgians from the Armenians, tells of the quarrel of the early 7th century under Kyrion and keeps the letters of Vrtanes Kertogh and others of that time. The third, on the baptism of the people called Tsad, is lost.

Here are the two surviving parts in Digilib's text, three parts; Digilib lists its sections out of order, so they were fetched in the order of their numbers.
