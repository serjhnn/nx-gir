# Aristakes Lastivertsi

years: c. 1002 – 1080
role: historian, vardapet
source: Wikipedia

He was born in the village of Lastiver, whose place is unknown. A vardapet, he knew theology, rhetoric and Greek.

In 1072–1079, without a patron, he wrote his History of the sufferings brought by the foreign peoples around us, as an eyewitness. In twenty-five chapters it tells of Bagratid Armenia's dealings with Byzantium and Georgia, the Seljuk invasions of 1047–1048, the fall of Ani in 1064 and the battle of Manzikert in 1071.
