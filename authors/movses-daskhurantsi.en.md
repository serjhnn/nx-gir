# Movses Daskhurantsi

years: 10th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia

The History of the Country of Aghvank, on Caucasian Albania and the eastern provinces of Armenia, is ascribed to him. The old manuscripts give no author: Mkhitar Gosh first named him Daskhurantsi, and Kirakos Gandzaketsi called him Kaghankatvatsi. The book may be the work of several authors.

Its first two books reach the 7th century, the Khazar invasions among other things, and keep the History of 684. The third book reaches the 10th century, and the last additions the 11th or 12th.
