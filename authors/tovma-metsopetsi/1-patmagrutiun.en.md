# History

written: first half of the 15th century
kind: a history of Timur and his successors
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The History of Timur and his successors, mostly an eyewitness's memories: Timur's campaigns, the Turkmen rulers of Tabriz, Shah-Mansur of Khorasan, the Georgian king Bagrat, and what Armenia and Georgia suffered in the 14th and 15th centuries.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 76 sections in one part. Digilib shows the last chapter again under the five appendix headings; those repeats are left empty here.
