# History of the Armenians

written: late 8th century
kind: a history of Arab rule in Armenia, 632 – 789
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Written at the request of Shapuh Bagratuni, from 774 on as an eyewitness. It begins with Muhammad and the first caliphs and tells of Arab rule in Armenia: the burning of the nobles in the churches of Nakhchivan and Khram, the revolts of 703, 748, 762 and 774–775, and the caliphs' governors. For 662–770 it is almost the only source.

The text was long thought lost and was found only in the 1840s. Here it is in Digilib's text, 44 sections in two parts; one is empty.
