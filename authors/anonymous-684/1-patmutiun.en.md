# History of 684

written: the 680s
kind: a history of Caucasian Albania
source: the preface of the Digilib edition

A history of Aghvank (Caucasian Albania) from the 630s to the events of 682–683: the deeds of the great prince Javanshir, the inroads of the northern peoples, the faith of the Huns' commander and the mission of Bishop Israel to the land of the Huns. It also keeps Davtak Kertogh's Lament on the death of Javanshir.

It has come down copied word for word into the History of the Aghvank of Movses Daskhurantsi, and the editor takes it out of that book, keeping its chapter numbers there ([ԻԷ], [ԺԸ] …). Here it is in Digilib's text, with the editor's preface in modern Armenian; two sections are empty on Digilib.
