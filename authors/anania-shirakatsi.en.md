# Anania Shirakatsi

years: c. 610 – 685
role: mathematician, astronomer, geographer
source: Wikipedia

Born in Shirak. He studied in Trebizond under Tychicus, then came back to Armenia and taught. He is the only classical Armenian author who wrote his own life.

He is called the father of the exact and natural sciences in Armenia. He wrote arithmetic textbooks with tables of the four operations, a book of puzzles for feasts (the Khrakhchanakank), and works on the calendar, the sky, clouds, measures and weights. The Ashkharhatsuyts, the first geography in Armenian, is also ascribed to him.
