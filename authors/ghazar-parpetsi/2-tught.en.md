# Letter

written: before around 493
kind: a letter of self-defence
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The Letter to Vahan Mamikonian. Called to Vagharshapat to oversee the rebuilding of a monastery, Ghazar was accused of heresy by the clergy there and left for Amida. From there he wrote to Vahan, defending himself and his learning and answering the charges of the monks against him.

It tells much about Ghazar's life and the learning of his time that his History does not. Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part.
