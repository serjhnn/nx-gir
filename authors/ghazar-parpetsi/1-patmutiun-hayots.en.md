# History of the Armenians

written: around 500
kind: a history of Armenia, 387 – 485
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Written at the request of Vahan Mamikonian, who called the author back from Amida around 493. In three books it runs from the partition of Armenia in 387 to 485, when Vahan was made marzpan: the end of the Arsacid kingdom, the war of Vardan Mamikonian and the battle of Avarayr in 451, and the revolt of Vahan that followed. With Yeghishe's book it is the main source on the war of Vardan.
