# Reflections on the Rites of the Church

written: late 12th century
kind: a commentary on the services
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

A commentary on the services of the Armenian Church, what the orders of the clergy, the prayers and the rites mean, with a reflection on the orthodox faith; parts were written at the request of priests. Its author, archbishop of Tarsus, was a champion of the union of the churches.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 133 sections in seven parts; Digilib lists its sections out of order, so they were fetched in the order of their numbers.
