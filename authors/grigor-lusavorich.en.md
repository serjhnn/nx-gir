# Gregory the Illuminator

years: c. 257 – c. 331
role: the first head of the Armenian Church
source: Wikipedia

He was the son of the Parthian noble Anak, who killed King Khosrov. The child Gregory was saved and raised as a Christian in Caesarea of Cappadocia. Back in Armenia he served King Trdat III, but refused to sacrifice to the goddess Anahit, and Trdat threw him into the pit of Khor Virap, where he stayed fourteen years.

Freed, he healed the king and baptized him, and in 301 Armenia took Christianity as the religion of the state. He is the founder and first head of the Armenian Church, and a saint. The Frequent Discourses (Yachakhapatum chark) and canons are ascribed to him.
