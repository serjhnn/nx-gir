# Komitas Aghtsetsi

years: catholicos 613 – 628
role: Catholicos of All Armenians, hymn writer
source: Wikipedia

Born in the village of Aghtsk, he was bishop of Taron. At a council called by King Khosrow II he set out the creed of the Armenian Church, and came back as catholicos. He is also called the Builder: he rebuilt the cathedral of Etchmiadzin, built the church of Saint Hripsime and the church of Saint Gregory in Dvin.

He took part in compiling the Seal of Faith (Knik havatoy), a collection of passages from the fathers of the Armenian and other churches in defence of the creed of the Armenian Church. He is also known as a writer of hymns.
