# Anania Targmanich

years: unknown
role: vardapet
source: the book itself

Digilib calls him Anania Targmanich (the Translator), and the book Anania vardapet. Which Anania he is, we do not know: there are several vardapets of that name in Armenian literature.

His work, On the Mystery of the Death of the Apostle Peter, explains Peter's words before the cross, when he asked to be crucified head down, and the mystery of the cross: a death that kills death and gives life.
