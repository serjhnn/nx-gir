# Canons

written: 5th century (catholicos 478 – 490)
kind: church canons
source: the book itself

"The canons of Hovhan Mandakuni, Catholicos of the Armenians", nine chapters on fasting and feasts: not to fast on the Saturdays and Sundays of Lent, not to kneel on Sundays and from Easter to Pentecost, when cheese, eggs, oil, fish and wine may be eaten, and the like.

Here they are in Digilib's text, one short part; the numbers in brackets are Digilib's notes, kept in the text.
