# On the Monasteries in Jerusalem

written: the 570s, by the editor's dating
kind: a list of monasteries
source: the preface of the Digilib edition

A list of the Armenian monasteries of Jerusalem, with a colophon that tells of the events of its time. It became known through the editions of Ghukas Inchichian and Ghevond Alishan; long placed in the 7th or 8th century and doubted by some, it is held genuine by the editor here, who dates it to the 570s.

The manuscripts are copies of the 17th to 19th centuries, in two versions. Here it is in Digilib's text, with the editor's preface in modern Armenian, one short part.
