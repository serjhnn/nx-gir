# History of the Armenians

written: 5th century, by its own account (some scholars place it in the 7th–9th)
kind: a history of Armenia from its beginnings
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The first attempt at a whole history of Armenia, written, the author says, at the request of Prince Sahak Bagratuni. In three books it runs from Hayk, the forefather of the Armenians, and the old kings through the Arsacids and the conversion to Christianity, to the death of Sahak Partev and Mesrop Mashtots in the 5th century, and ends with a lament over Armenia. It keeps old oral tales and songs that are known from nowhere else.

The book earned its author the name Father of History and shaped every Armenian historian after him. Here it is in Digilib's text, 194 sections in three parts; Digilib sorts the sections by title, so they were fetched in the order of their numbers.
