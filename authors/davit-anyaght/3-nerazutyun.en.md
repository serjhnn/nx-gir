# Commentary on Porphyry's Introduction

written: 6th century (in Greek; translated into Armenian)
kind: a commentary on Porphyry
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

David's commentary on the Isagoge, Porphyry's introduction to Aristotle's logic, which explains genus, species, difference, property and accident, in twenty-nine lectures (prakk).

Its Greek original survives beside the Armenian. Here it is in Digilib's text, one part; Digilib marks it "mixed", with a modern foreword or notes beside the Grabar, and one section is empty there.
