# Definitions and Divisions of Philosophy

written: 6th century (in Greek; translated into Armenian)
kind: an introduction to philosophy
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

A handbook for beginners, given as lectures (prakk): what philosophy is, its definitions and why there are so many, and how it divides into theory and practice. It follows the Neoplatonic teaching of Alexandria, where David studied under Olympiodorus.

The Greek original is kept too; the Armenian translation belongs to the Hellenizing school of the 6th to 8th centuries. Here it is in Digilib's text, one part; Digilib marks it "mixed", with a modern foreword or notes beside the Grabar, and two sections are empty there.
