# Commentary on Aristotle's Categories

written: 6th century (in Greek; translated into Armenian)
kind: a commentary on Aristotle
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

A commentary on Aristotle's Categories, the ten kinds of things that can be said of a subject, in thirty-eight lectures (prakk). It is ascribed to David; scholars also link it with Elias, another pupil of Olympiodorus.

Here it is in Digilib's text, 36 sections in two parts.
