# On Aristotle's Analytics

written: 6th century (in Greek; translated into Armenian)
kind: a commentary on Aristotle
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

David's commentary on Aristotle's Prior Analytics, the book on the syllogism, in fourteen lectures (prakk). Its Greek original is lost, and it survives only in the Armenian.

It is one of the handbooks by which Armenian schools learned logic for centuries. Here it is in Digilib's text, one part; Digilib marks it "mixed", with a modern foreword or notes beside the Grabar.
