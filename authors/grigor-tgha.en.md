# Grigor Tgha

years: c. 1133 – 1193
role: Catholicos of All Armenians, poet
source: Wikipedia

A nephew of Nerses Shnorhali, he was Catholicos of All Armenians from 1173 to 1193. He was called Tgha, "the Young", though he was about forty when he took the see. He kept to his uncles' policy of drawing near to the other churches, and after the death of Manuel Komnenos turned to Pope Lucius III.

He wrote poems and theological works, among them the Lament for Jerusalem, on the fall of Jerusalem to Saladin in 1187. His Letters are in the Library. He died in 1193 after a fall from his horse.
