# Hovhan Mamikonean

years: 7th century
role: historian
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

The History of Taron, a continuation of the account of Zenob Glak, has come down under his name. In its colophon he calls himself abbot and bishop of the monastery of Surb Karapet, the thirty-fifth after Zenob, and says that he wrote under Emperor Heraclius and King Khosrow, at the request of Catholicos Nerses the Builder.

The first part tells how the new faith spread in Taron in the 4th century, the second the heroic struggle of the Mamikonians and the Armenians of Taron. Scholars think the book was put together or edited between the 7th and the 9th centuries.
