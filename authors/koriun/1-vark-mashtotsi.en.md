# Life of Mashtots

written: 443 – 451
kind: a life of Mesrop Mashtots
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The oldest original work written in Armenian: the life of Mesrop Mashtots by his youngest pupil, begun after his death in 440, perhaps for its third anniversary in 443. It tells how Mashtots invented the Armenian letters, how he preached and taught, and how his pupils translated the Bible and other books from Greek and Syriac.

Digilib gives the text four times, in four editions one after another: Manuk Abeghian's and A. Matevosian's (Yerevan), Poghos Ananian's (Venice, San Lazzaro) and G. Fndglian's (Jerusalem, 1930), the first three in 29 chapters. They are kept as Digilib has them, in two parts.
