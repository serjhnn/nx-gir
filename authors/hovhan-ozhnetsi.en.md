# Hovhan Odznetsi the Philosopher

years: catholicos 717 – 728
role: Catholicos of All Armenians, theologian
source: Wikipedia

Born in Odzun, he studied under Theodoros Kurtenavor, who had written against the Docetists. He was Catholicos of All Armenians from 717 to 728 and is called the Philosopher. He kept good relations with Caliph Umar II and won tax exemptions for the Church.

His main work is the Book of Canons of the Armenians (Kanonagirk Hayots), the first ordered collection of church law. He also wrote homilies and theological works against those who held Christ's body to be only an appearance.
