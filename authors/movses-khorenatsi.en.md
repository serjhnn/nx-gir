# Movses Khorenatsi

years: 5th century
role: historian, translator
source: Wikipedia

He presents himself as a young pupil of Mesrop Mashtots who wrote at the request of Prince Sahak Bagratuni. When he really lived has been debated since the 19th century: some scholars place him in the 7th to 9th centuries. The Armenian Church counts him among the Holy Translators.

His History of the Armenians is the first attempt at a whole history of Armenia, from its beginnings to the 5th century, and it keeps old Armenian oral traditions too. That is why he is called the Father of History. The book shaped all later Armenian historians.
