# Chronicle

written: 14th century
kind: a short chronicle
source: the book itself

A short list of years from 1187, when Saladin took Jerusalem, through the Khwarazmians and the Mongol conquest of Greater Armenia in 1236 and on, one event to a line. Each year is written twice, in figures and in Armenian letters.

It is in Middle Armenian, the language of the 12th to 14th centuries, rather than Grabar. Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part.
