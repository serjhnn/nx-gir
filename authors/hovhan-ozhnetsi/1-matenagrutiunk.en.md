# Works

written: early 8th century (catholicos 717 – 728)
kind: collected works
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

The works of Hovhan the Philosopher gathered in one book: a discourse that opens with the patience of Job, canons on the life of the clergy ("a bishop, priest or deacon found drunk …"), a discourse against those who held Christ's body to be only an appearance, and a long explanation of the services of the Church, of what the prayers of the evening, the morning and the third, sixth and ninth hours mean, with homilies.

He is best known for the Book of Canons of the Armenians, the first ordered collection of church law, which is not in this book. Here it is in Digilib's text, two parts.
