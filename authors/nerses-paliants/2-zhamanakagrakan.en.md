# Chronological Fragments

written: 14th century
kind: fragments of a chronicle
source: Wikipedia · the book itself

Pieces of Nerses's history of the kings of Cilicia, above all of their dealings with the Mongols, taken from a manuscript whose page numbers stand in the text (/17բ/). It is a valuable but often unreliable source, and parts of it were later added to Smbat Sparapet's Chronicle.

Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part.
