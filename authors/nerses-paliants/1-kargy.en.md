# The Order of the Kings and Princes of Armenia

written: 1351
kind: a list of the rulers of Armenia
source: the book itself

The princes and kings of Armenia in their order, written, the author says, as a book of the succession of rulers: from the first kings, with the disagreements of the sources about them, to the kings of Cilicia and King Constantine, who reigns "now", in 1351.
