# Vrtanes Kertogh

years: mid-6th – early 7th century
role: writer, grammarian, theologian
source: Wikipedia · Armenian Wikipedia

He studied at the school of the catholicosate in Dvin, and his fine style earned him the name Kertogh, "the grammarian". In 589 he went with the vardapet Grigor to a church council in Constantinople as the envoy of Catholicos Movses II, to defend the creed of the Armenian Church. When the catholicos died in 604, he led the Church until Abraham Aghbatanetsi was elected.

The quarrel between the Armenian and Georgian churches began in his time; his letters are kept in the Book of Letters and in the history of Ukhtanes. His main work, On the Iconoclasts (Yaghags patkeramartits), defends holy images against those who rejected them.
