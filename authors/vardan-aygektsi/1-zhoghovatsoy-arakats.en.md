# Collection of Fables

written: early 13th century
kind: fables
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Vardan's fables: short tales of kings, men and beasts, the lion and the hunters, the lion that feigned sickness, the lion and the fox, the sick lion and the donkey without a heart, each with its moral. The fox is the hero of so many that the collection printed in Amsterdam in 1668 was called the Fox Book.

Robert Sahakyants made the cartoon The Fox Book from them in 1975. Here they are in Digilib's text, 389 sections in three parts; the Contents are read from their lettered titles.
