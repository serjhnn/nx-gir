# Anastas Vardapet

years: 6th century (?)
role: vardapet
source: the preface of the Digilib edition

All we know of him is that the list of the Armenian monasteries of Jerusalem, On the Monasteries in Jerusalem, with its colophon, has come down under his name.

The list became known in the 19th century through the editions of Ghukas Inchichian and Ghevond Alishan. It was traditionally placed in the 7th or 8th century, and some doubted that it was genuine, but the preface of the edition argues that it is, and dates it to the 570s.
