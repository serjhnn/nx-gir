# Mkhitar Gosh

years: c. 1130 – 1213
role: vardapet, jurist, fabulist
source: Wikipedia

Born in Gandzak. He studied in Cilicia, in the monasteries of the Black Mountain, then came back to northern Armenia and, with the help of the Zakarian princes, founded the monastery of Nor Getik, known today after him as Goshavank. He is buried there.

In 1184 he began the Datastanagirk (Book of Law), the first Armenian code of both civil and church law. Later Armenian law codes were built on it, Smbat Sparapet's among them, and the Armenians of Poland lived by it until 1772. He also wrote fables.
