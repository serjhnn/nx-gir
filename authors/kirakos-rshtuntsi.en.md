# Kirakos Rshtuntsi

years: 15th century
role: chronicler, vardapet
source: the book itself

A vardapet, from Rshtunik by his name. We know of him mostly from his chronicle: in 1443, when Catholicos Kirakos was deposed, he and the vardapet Sargis were beaten too, as he writes himself.

His short Chronicle lists, year by year, the reigns of the Mongol khans and the raids of Timur and of the Turkmens around Van, Archesh and Baghesh, from the 13th century to the 1440s.
