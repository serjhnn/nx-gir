# On the Iconoclasts

written: around 600
kind: a treatise in defence of images
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Written against an Armenian sect that rejected images in churches. Vrtanes defends them by the pictures in Armenian churches, the Virgin with the Child and the saints, and the scenes of Christ's life, "the Nativity, Baptism, Passion and Crucifixion, the Burial, Resurrection and Ascension", and by the example of Moses, who made the images of the cherubim.

If it is his, it is the earliest surviving work written against iconoclasm, a century before the quarrel over images in Byzantium. Here it is in Digilib's text, one short part, in numbered verses.
