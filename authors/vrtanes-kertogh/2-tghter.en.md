# Letters

written: around 604 – 607
kind: letters
source: Wikipedia · the Digilib edition

Seven letters from the time when Vrtanes led the Armenian Church after the death of Catholicos Movses II, when the Georgian Church under Kyrion drew away from the Armenian: the answer to the letter of Sormen, a circular letter to the churches of Tsurtav, a letter to Peter the Georgian, and three answers to Bishop Movses of Tsurtav, who was pressed by Kyrion and fled.

They show the start of the break between the two churches. Many of his letters were kept in the History of Ukhtanes and in the Book of Letters. Here they are in Digilib's text, one part.
